# Delves and sieges

**Status:** Vision · interactive battle stage shared with them **WIP**

**Loops:** dungeon crawler (Delve) · farm, hunt, and defend (Siege is the defend beat) · quests inside delves. See [The loops](the-loops.md).

---

## Two places you go to act

Most of the UI is a **layer** — you open Creatures or the Almanac over wherever you already are. A few places are **stages**: you travel there to *do* something, not only to look.

Besides Sanctum, World, and Lawn, two more stages are locked in as fiction:

| Stage | Fantasy | Loop |
|---|---|---|
| **Delve** | A party crawl through seeded rooms toward a boss — haul out or fail trying | Hunt / dungeon crawl |
| **Siege** | Defend or assault the Seat on a tactical board — buildings as actors, occupy to possess | Defend the empire |

Neither is in the current loop. Both will return you to the Sanctum when they resolve. Expeditions stay the idle sibling — they are not replaced by delves.
---

## The Delve (**Vision**)

You find **domains** out in the world (expedition discoveries are part of that story). From the Sanctum you pick a found domain and enter.

Inside: a room graph. Fights, elites, caches, shrines, merchants, traps, wild talk. You manage a **pack**. You **extract** with a haul — or you wipe.

### Pack, extract, oaths, domains

| Piece | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Domains** | Named places you find, then enter from the Sanctum |
| **Pack** | The party you take into the room graph |
| **Extract** | Leave with the haul — or fail trying |
| **Oaths / permadeath** | Optional hard stakes on difficulty rungs |

Nerve and spirit pressure belong here — Unsettled → Shaken → Afflicted as the delve gets under your skin. Wild talk can open recruit or capture paths.

**Unlock (when it ships):** first domain found on an expedition.

### Formations and combo skills (**Vision**)

A second axis besides who is in the pack: **how they stand**, and a few **combination skills**. Matter when you play the Delve or an interactive battle. Expeditions stay auto-resolve. See [The loops](the-loops.md).

### Failure branches (**Vision**)

A wipe or a failed extract can open different ground — a new domain, a quest, bandits on the map — not only a dead run. Consequence as content. Shared with the farm / hunt / defend loop.

---

## Siege (**Vision**)

When the war hits the Seat — yours or theirs — the fight is not only a graph node. It becomes a **siege board**: structures, lanes of approach, possession of buildings.

Defend the homeworld. Assault Zomboss’s fortress when you find it. Outcome feeds back to the rift.

## Battles in the middle

**WIP** before delves feel finished: an interactive **battle** stage — initiative track, action bar, targets you choose — so fights on the map (and later in rooms) are something you play, not only something that resolves in the dark.

Expeditions stay the async loop. Battles, delves, and sieges are where presence matters.
