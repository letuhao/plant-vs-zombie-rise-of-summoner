# Sanctum

**Status:** Shipped (thin) · first-run naming ritual **WIP**

Home between every place loop. Full loop list: [The loops](the-loops.md).

---

## What it is

The Sanctum is your **home stage** — the summoner’s hall. When no run is in progress, you are here. When a lawn, battle, delve, or siege ends, you return here.

It is somewhere to *be*, not a menu of places to go. Creatures on display, a map table for travel, a pact shelf, a fusion altar, an expedition board, and a focus card that answers “what next?”

---

## What you do here

- See soul balance and summoner level on the HUD
- Open any layer from the rail without leaving the hall
- Travel to the **world map** (`M`, or the map table)
- Start a lawn run when a match is ready
- Collect expedition returns and read results that came back while you were away

Time in the Sanctum does not push you. Expedition timers are wall-clock and shown as remaining — not ticking pressure.

---

## First-run naming ritual (**WIP**)

On a brand-new save, the hall starts almost empty on purpose. You bind your first creature **in place**, give it a name, and get one clear next step — not a tour of every menu.

Until that ritual ships, you still land in the Sanctum and use the rail yourself.

## Layers over the Sanctum

These open over the hall (and over every other stage) and close back to where you were:

| Layer | Key | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Creatures | `C` | Roster — bind, rename, equip, deploy, release |
| Commanders | `K` | Who leads the next lawn run |
| Relics | `R` | Gear vault |
| Fusion | `F` | Merge specimens; browse recipes |
| Pacts | `P` | Contracts, loyalty, tribute |
| Expeditions | `E` | Dispatch and collect |
| Almanac | `A` | Reference dossiers |
| Chronicle | `H` | Run history and progression |
| System | `Esc` on empty stack | Settings, keybinds, display, quit |

### Unlock ladder

| Layer | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| Creatures, Commanders | Session start |
| Relics | When you hold your first item |
| Fusion | Once you have a demon to fuse |
| Pacts | When a contract is first offered |
| Expeditions | Once you have a bound demon to field |
| Almanac, Chronicle | After your first run |

Delve travel (**Vision**) unlocks when you have found a domain.

---

## Settings and keymap

**System** (Esc on an empty stack) is where you rebind keys, adjust display, and quit. The keymap matches the verb table on this page — pressing `?` should always agree with what the rail does.

---

## Shell before the hall

Boot → title → **save select**. A save is chosen at the door, not from a dropdown wedged into every screen.
