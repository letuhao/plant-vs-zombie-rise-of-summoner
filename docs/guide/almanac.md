# Almanac

**Status:** Shipped (thin) — dossiers, recipes, chronicle shells grow as you play

**Loop:** spine A — level up and power (chronicle, type XP). See [The loops](the-loops.md).

---

## A record of your playthrough

See something in the game and the RPG files it: portrait, pedia text, cost, type information. What you meet becomes a browsable set of **dossiers**. Fusion recipes you uncover sit beside them.

You end up with a record of **your** run through the war — not a wiki somebody else wrote.

---

## Almanac layer (`A`)

Reference material, open over any stage:

- Creature types you have discovered
- Elements and the ring
- Statuses
- Recipes
- Effect families as they become player-visible

Discovery states matter. An empty slot is something you have not met yet — not a broken link.

Codex milestones for demons reward exploration. Web-only players can still claim completion milestones without every exclusive lawn capture.

---

## Chronicle layer (`H`)

Your history:

- Run results
- Progression dossier
- XP ledgers for the summoner, plant types, zombie types, and specimens
- The lasting sheet of what you have earned and spent

Progress **survives the run**. Nothing you earn disappears with the level. There is no hard ceiling that says the grind is over — magnitudes keep meaning as you climb.

---

## How progression lands

| Track | What grows |
|---|---|
| Summoner | Your account level and XP |
| Plant / zombie types | Per-type levels from play |
| Specimens | Individual demons from work they did — lawn, expedition, battle |

Souls are separate from XP: souls buy summons and rituals; XP grows power curves and unlocks.

---

## In this build

Almanac capture and chronicle surfaces exist. Some dossiers are still thin; presentation polish continues. Older “types / recipes / runs” routes redirect into these layers as the Sanctum model finishes replacing the old audit screens.

Developer dump pages are not the almanac. If it looks like a raw export, it is not for players.
