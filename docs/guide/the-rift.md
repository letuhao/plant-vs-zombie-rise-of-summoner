# The rift

**Status:** Shipped (map, End Turn, fog, Zomboss, loam as ground) · sector buildings, map tools, map→battle handoff **WIP** · three-layer world, world generator, world events, full world stage **Vision**

**Loops:** world-map adventure · world-stage empire · farm, hunt, and defend. See [The loops](the-loops.md).

---

## What it is

The **rift** is where the war is played — a network of **sectors** joined by lanes. You march **legions**, claim ground, raise economy and defences, and fight for control.

You are Dave. The capital is his **homeworld**. Find Zomboss’s fortress and take it before yours falls.

Time on the map is **turn-based** — **virtual turns**, already built. Everyone commits orders; then the turn resolves for every commander at once. There is no clock bullying you — the barrier waits until you (and Zomboss, and the neutrals) are ready. Press **End Turn** when your orders are set. This is not a real-life diary clock.

## World map — adventure

Where you **go**. Scout and expand through **fog**. Your own ground is clear; other people’s ground and the past stay uncertain.

Holding a sector grants full sight of it — standing on your own land is never a guessing game. Fog is about other people’s ground and about memory.

Zomboss evaluates from **his** fog. He does not peek at your save.

What you do on the adventure graph:

- March legions along lanes; claim sectors; watch supply and contact
- Hold what you take — ground is not free to keep
- Read Zomboss as a real opponent
- Later: doors into delves, cede, dowse, lenses, outliner (**WIP** / **Vision**)

## World stage — empire building

Where you **are** when you run the empire. The product vision is a place you stand in — one camera, corner HUD, loam strip, calendar, inspector over the map — not a flowchart with a sidebar of buttons.

Inside a held sector (**Vision** / **WIP** as it hardens): slots, buildings, recruitment, upkeep, wardens. Neglect makes the Fracture real.

**In this build:** the graph and End Turn are playable; the full stage surface (HUD, inspector, recruitment as a game) is still ahead. See [The loops](the-loops.md) place 5.

## Loam and the Fracture

Every sector carries a **loam** story.

- Fertile land supports an expanding empire
- Barren land drains one
- Territory you neglect **fades**, then is **lost** to the Fracture

**Loam** is a world stock — it buys position and buildings. It never banks into the next world. **Souls** and **essence** do.

Nothing outside your anchored ground is really there. A holding without a working loam source fades gradually and visibly. Structures die with the fade; natural rootbeds remain as strategic features you can found on again.

---

## Three stocks

| Stock | Buys | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Loam** | Holding and building ground | This world only |
| **Souls** | Roster power — summons, contracts, rituals | Player — banks |
| **Essence** (per element) | Fusion and some buildings | Player — banks |

You keep who you are. You lose where you were.

## Layers of the war (**Vision**)

The full fantasy is three layers:

1. **Rift graph** — sectors and lanes (what you play today) — adventure
2. **Sector board** — inside one sector: slots, construction, your base — empire stage
3. **Lane board** — five-lane combat when a fight is tested

**Vision:** procedural **world generator**, deeper fog craft, full three-layer presentation, **world events and raids**, **enemy counter-development** (the war shifts from how you play), and **failure branches** (loss can open different ground). See [The loops](the-loops.md).
