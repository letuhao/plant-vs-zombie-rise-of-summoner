## You have no class

Character building is **free**. Points go wherever you want, at one price. There is no posture gate and no surcharge for dabbling. That is the **power** loop — Dave’s level, aptitudes, later trees — not a class pick.
**Classes** still exist — as **Zomboss AI patterns**. Named enemy builds make an opponent legible. They are not containers you pick for yourself.

What you *do* have:

- **Twelve aptitudes** — the primary stats of a build
- **Postures** — Force, Finesse, Bastion — regions of build space, not locks
- **Relics** — gear in one vault
- **Species builds** (**WIP**) — species you field grow their own lean
- **Passive trees** (**Vision**) — spend souls into trees for identity, reward, and risk
- **Commanders** — who leads the lawn run
- **Commander auras** (**WIP**) — side-wide presence from that leader

## Free-build aptitudes (**Shipped**)

Spend commander points across the twelve. What you allocate reaches every demon you field.

| Aptitude | Feel |
|---|---|
| Might | Hit harder |
| Fortitude | Take less of everything |
| Vigor | More to lose before you lose |
| Onslaught | Their guard stops mattering |
| Agility | Be somewhere else |
| Composure | Nothing lands clean on you |
| Pierce | Armour stops mattering |
| Focus | Do it again, sooner, cheaper |
| Bulwark | Stop it outright, sometimes |
| Retribution | Hitting you costs them |
| Precision | They cannot dodge |
| Ferocity | Sometimes it is much worse |

Allocation can stack across commander, species, variant, and the unique demon itself — who you are is the sum of those layers. Priced **respec** is the friction that holds a build together.

**In this build:** commander-scope spend is live and thin. Other scopes and a polished sheet are still ahead.

## Species builds (**WIP**)

Species you field grow their own aptitude lean over time. You can override; changing your mind costs a priced respec. Zomboss can show adaptive patterns after fights — content, not a player class.

---

## Relics armoury and crafting (**WIP**)

This is the **item collection and progression** loop: find, vault, equip, compare, craft, socket, salvage.

One player vault. Equip. Compare. Salvage what you do not need.

**Vision:** the armoury opens as a chapter when Dave reaches the item unlock (proposed level 10). Drops before that either do not roll or bank until the vault opens — no dead loot UI.

**Relic crafting** is the deep gear loop:
- Rolled gear with affixes frozen at roll
- Sets and charms
- Sockets and combinations
- Enhance and reroll that cost something real instead of pure luck
- Named uniques that break the generator’s rules
- Consumables for rest and fights

Until the armoury is a real loop, older storage surfaces may still exist as thin pages. Treat them as temporary scaffolding, not the fantasy.

Rarity reads in horticultural names — from Chaff up through Almanac. See the [glossary](glossary.md).

## Passive trees (**Vision**)

Trees are how a build gets a face. Many trees can be equal in expected value; concentrating buys reward and risk. Respec costs souls. Catalogs are learnable and shared — you are not locked into a secret wiki.

## Build presets (**Vision**)

Save a **synergy loadout** — patron, relics, aptitudes, who you field — so a fire lean is something you keep, not five independent clicks. Hangs on the item and power loops. See [The loops](the-loops.md).

## Commanders and auras (**WIP**)
