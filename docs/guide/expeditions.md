# Expeditions

**Status:** Shipped · interactive battles **WIP** · ticks that start quests and world events **Vision**

**Loop:** place 2 — idle expeditions, core forever. See [The loops](the-loops.md).

---

## What they are

Expeditions are the **idle RPG** loop — Monster Hunter–style dispatch that stays forever. Playable with the lawn game closed. Sibling of the Delve, not a prototype to replace.

Pick demons who are not on a live task. Dispatch them. Wait. Collect.

Durations are built for real schedules:

| Tier | Length |
|---|---|
| Short | 30 minutes |
| Medium | 4 hours |
| Long | 8 hours |
| Overnight | 20 hours |

Twenty hours so daily schedule drift never punishes you. There is no stamina gate — with no monetization, a stamina wall has no honest job.

---

## Recall and slots

Parallelism is gated by **expedition slots** — you grow from a few to more as you progress.

**Recall anytime.** Rewards pro-rate to completed ticks — leave early, take what you earned so far. Nothing expires if you forget to collect.

---

## What happens while they are out

Each tier resolves a **battle chain** interleaved with event ticks:

- Quiet stretches
- Found souls
- Meeting a wild demon
- Injuries

Outcome is sealed at dispatch by a recorded seed and revealed at collection.

**Vision:** ticks that **start quests** and **world events**, and discoveries that open domains for the Delve.

---

## What you bring home

- **Souls** and summoner XP (power loop)
- **Specimen XP** for demons who fought
- A chance a defeated wave demon **joins** the roster (summon loop — a non-gacha path)
- Fusion **materials** (essence) for later merges and for empire buildings that ask for them

Materials feed **actors and empire** — never loam. Loam never banks.

Start short. Grow into overnight runs when the roster can carry them.

---

## How this sits next to battles and delves

Expeditions auto-resolve. **WIP:** interactive turn-based **battles** you play yourself — same combat language, your hands on the action bar. Expeditions stay the async loop; battles become the tense one.

Delves and sieges are separate stages — see [Delves and sieges](delves-and-sieges.md). Expeditions do not become delves.

---

## In this build

Dispatch → wait → collect is live. Wild joins, materials, recall, and the four duration tiers are part of the loop. The Expeditions layer opens from the Sanctum rail (`E`) once you have a **bound demon** to field. Quest- and event-starting ticks stay **Vision**.
