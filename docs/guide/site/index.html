RPG · empire · local on your machine

An RPG and empire war that grows from your lawn matches

Rise of Summoner is an RPG plus empire-building game. You raise demons, run idle expeditions, and fight for ground across a fractured multiverse. You play as Crazy Dave. Everything runs on your machine — no account, no cloud.

What this is

A persistent summoner RPG and empire campaign. Matches on the board feed souls, levels, and your roster. Away from the board you summon, fuse specimens, dispatch idle squads, and march a war map. The lawn is the first core loop — not the whole game.

What you need

Plants vs. Zombies: Fusion is a fan-made Plants vs. Zombies game (separate from EA’s official titles). You point the Rise of Summoner launcher at a legal install of that pack.

Rise of Summoner does not replace it. It adds the RPG and empire layer on top. In this guide, the lawn means those matches — the live board where plants and zombies fight.

How a session feels

  1. Play a lawn match — souls, almanac, and deploy progress land in your save
  2. Raise demons at home — summon, bind pacts, fuse specimens
  3. Dispatch idle expeditions while you do something else
  4. March the rift map — claim ground, hold loam, End Turn against Zomboss

The full named loop list lives under The loops.

Win and lose

Win by finding Zomboss’s fortress and taking it. Lose if the homeworld falls. You keep who you are; you lose where you were.

Ten named loops

Every feature hangs on one of these. Spine is what you always grow. Places are where you play. No eleventh loop — and no diary meta-clock.

Play the lawn → grow power, summon, and gear → dispatch idle expeditions → farm, hunt, and defend the empire → adventure the map and build the stage → delve and quest → do it again on ground that fights back.

Spine — always on

  • Level up and power — Dave’s level has no cap; free-build aptitudes; you have no class
  • Summon and fusion — souls, altar, pacts, fusion; gacha is one path, never the only one
  • Item collection — find, vault, equip, craft relics as chapters open

Places — where you play

  • Lawn — first core; feeds almost everything
  • Idle expeditions — dispatch, wait, collect; no stamina; forever
  • Farm / hunt / defend — yield, prey, homeworld
  • World map — adventure; End Turn virtual turns
  • World stage — where you stand to run the empire
  • Delve — party crawl (coming)
  • Quests and events — story clock, not only loot

Three clocks

  • Lawn — live lawn match
  • Expeditions — wall-clock (30 min–20 h) on purpose
  • World — virtual turns; press End Turn

How you play

Local control room on your machine. No account. No cloud. You start on the lawn, then the rest of the war opens around it.

  1. Lawn — play a lawn match. Souls, experience, and almanac entries land in your save. Deploy demons you raised.
  2. Power — Dave levels. Specimens grow from work they did. Spend points into free-build aptitudes.
  3. Summon and fuse — altar, pacts, fusion. Keep demons loyal.
  4. Idle — dispatch spare demons on expeditions; collect later. No stamina.
  5. Empire — march the map, farm and hunt ground, hold loam, defend the Seat when the war reaches home.
  6. Return home — every run ends at the Sanctum. Check what came back. Go again.

Win and lose

You are Crazy Dave. Your capital is his homeworld — the one timeline still yours. Find Zomboss through the rift before yours falls.

Win

Find Zomboss’s fortress sector and take it.

Lose

The homeworld falls.

You keep who you are. You lose where you were.

Roster, souls, and essence bank across worlds. Loam and the holdings of a failed map do not. Ending one world and starting another is the progression loop — not a soft reset of your summoner.
