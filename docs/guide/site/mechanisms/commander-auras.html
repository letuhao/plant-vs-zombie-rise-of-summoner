Commander auras

WIP: a continuous side-wide presence (or Focus on your own actions) from the commander you chose — one aura active at a time.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Aura
Ongoing presence from the leader — not a one-shot skill click.
One at a time
Design is a single active aura, not a stack of every commander buff.
WIP
Not finished on the live lawn path.
Focus
Some auras may emphasize your own actions rather than only side-wide buffs — read the aura when it ships.
  • Set the leader first — commanders.
  • Hero recruitment into the commander slot is later fantasy on the same pillar.

What it will be

The commander you set carries one aura into the match.

It stays continuous for that snapshot.

What you do today

Pick a commander.

Play without expecting a finished aura chrome.

What you do while it is WIP

  1. Use Commanders (K) for who leads.
  2. Ignore missing aura readouts.
  3. Return when aura UI ships.

Gear loop: relics armoury.

Common mix-ups

Can I stack three auras?
No — one active at a time.
Is aura the same as patron colour?
No. Patron is element identity; aura is commander presence.
Broken lawn without aura?
No — WIP.
Does aura persist after the match?
It is match presence from the leader snapshot — not a permanent world buff.
