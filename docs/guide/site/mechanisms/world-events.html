Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Vision
Not playable yet. Do not expect blood moons or raid ecology on the live map today.
World event
A scheduled or triggered push from the world — not a single loot line in an expedition report.
Raid
A harder world push / boss pressure in that ecology — still Vision here.
Ecology
The living pressure of the war — sibling to counter-development.
