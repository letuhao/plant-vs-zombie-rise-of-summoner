Six elements and omni

Elements are real matchups — fire, ice, air, earth, light, dark, plus omni as the all-elements baseline — not flavour labels on a sheet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Element
A combat type on the creature and the hit. Six concrete colours plus omni — not a class and not a rarity.
The ring
The cycle fire → ice → earth → air → fire. That is the matchup story for those four.
Omni
The baseline “all elements” case — not a seventh colour you farm like fire essence.
Two types
A creature can carry up to two elements. Traits like void or chaos are traits — they are not extra ring colours.
Neutral
Cross pairs outside the ring story (and outside light↔dark) stay neutral — no invented third wheel.
  • The same ring reads on the lawn and in later battles — one fight language.
  • Essence colours line up with these elements when you fuse — see essence.

What it is

Six concrete elements, plus omni:

Fire · ice · air · earth · light · dark

Matchups are real numbers in combat feedback. Raising a demon’s element at home means something when you field it.

How the ring works

Four colours chase each other. Light and dark stand aside:

  fire -> ice -> earth -> air -> fire
  light <-> dark
  other crosses stay neutral
  1. Fire

    Melts ice.

  2. Ice

    Cracks earth.

  3. Earth

    Blocks air.

  4. Air

    Blows out fire.

Pair Story
Fire → ice → earth → air → fire The ring
Light ↔ dark Counter each other only
Everything else Neutral

This is not a second ring and invents no new elements. Named combo recipes on top of it are Vision.

Where you find it

On sheets and in fights

Element shows wherever combat and roster care.

  1. Open a specimen sheet and read its element(s).
  2. Field and fight with matchups in mind — lawn or web battle.
  3. Watch combat feedback when a hit lands soft or hard for the colour story.

Exact multipliers are balance surface. Trust what the UI shows that day.

What you do (first time)

  1. Pick a specimen and name its element out loud.
  2. Remember the ring: fire melts ice, ice cracks earth, earth blocks air, air blows out fire.
  3. Treat light and dark as a private duel — not part of that four-colour chase.

Common mix-ups

Is omni a seventh essence colour?
No. Omni is the baseline all-elements case in combat. Essence stock is the six colours — see essence.
Do void or chaos sit on the ring?
No. Those are traits, not extra elements.
Does the lawn use a different type chart?
No. Lawn and later battles share this combat language.
Can a creature have more than two elements?
No. Up to two types. Beyond that you are looking at traits or statuses.
Is this the same as named reactions?
No. The ring is shipped matchups. Named recipes on top of it are Vision — see named reactions.
