Shipped (fiction) Quests, failure, memory · prestige of place

New world — keep who you are

You keep who you are. You lose where you were. Ending a world is progression — not a prestige wipe of the summoner.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

New world
Starting another map after win or lose. Roster, souls, and essence come with you; loam and that map’s holdings do not.
Fiction status
The rule is real product law in the guide. Surfaces that start/end worlds may still feel thin — the promise is the keep/lose split.
Prestige wipe
The rejected idea that a new world deletes your summoner power. This game does not do that.
Loam stays behind
World stock and failed-map holdings die with the place. They never bank.
  • Win: take Zomboss’s fortress. Lose: the homeworld falls.
  • Same roster, same souls, same essence — whichever world you open next.

What it is

Ending one world and starting another *is* the progression loop.

Identity (roster, souls, essence, Dave’s power) banks. Place (loam, holdings) resets with the map.

Keeps Leaves behind
Roster / specimens Loam
Souls Holdings of the failed or finished map
Essence That world’s ground story

Status is Shipped (fiction) — the law holds even when world-end chrome is thin.

Why it exists

Without a keep/lose split, empire play either wipes your summoner (punishing) or banks forever-ground (boring).

You keep who you are so power and roster mean something across maps. You lose where you were so holdings stay a real fight.

  win or lose world
        |
        +->  keep: roster, souls, essence, Dave power
        +->  lose: loam, holdings, that map

Where you meet it

Win and lose

Defined on The game — fortress vs homeworld.

  1. Play toward Zomboss’s fortress — or fall defending the homeworld.
  2. When a world ends, expect banked identity and a fresh place.
  3. Open Chronicle / Creatures to confirm who you still are.

Exact end-of-world UI may be thin; the keep/lose rule is the teachable core.

What you do (first time)

  1. Read win/lose on The game once.
  2. Check that souls and essence are player banks — loam is not.
  3. Treat a future new-world start as prestige of place, not a summoner delete.

Stop when new world ≠ soft reset of Dave. Related: loam, souls.

Common mix-ups

Does a new world delete my demons?
No. Roster banks. You lose the map’s holdings and loam.
Do souls reset?
No. Souls and essence bank across worlds.
Can I keep loam?
No. Loam never banks.
Is this a paid prestige layer?
No. Play is local with no payments — new world is the place loop.
Why “fiction” on a Shipped badge?
The keep/lose law is product-real; world-end presentation can still feel thin.
