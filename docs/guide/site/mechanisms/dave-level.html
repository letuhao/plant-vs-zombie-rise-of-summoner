Shipped Power and builds · level up and power

Dave's level

Dave’s level is the main power line for your summoner — endless, with no cap. Play raises it; Vision chapters may drip complexity by it later.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Dave’s level
Your summoner’s level — Crazy Dave’s power line — not a lawn plant’s XP bar alone.
Endless / no cap
There is no soft ceiling where grinding stops mattering by design. New worlds raise prestige; they do not wipe who you are.
Aptitude points
Spendable power you earn as Dave grows — see free-build aptitudes.
Chapters
Vision drip by Dave level — not the live rail today. See unlock chapters.
  • Specimens and type lines also grow from work they did — see type and specimen XP.
  • HUD and Chronicle show progression; exact curves live in balance data.

What it is

The spine of the power loop: play → Dave levels → spend into builds.

No designed hard stop where more play stops counting.

How you use it

  1. Play lawn, expeditions, and map work that feed progression.
  2. Watch Dave’s level on the HUD / Chronicle.
  3. Spend aptitude points as you earn them — there is no soft ceiling telling you to stop.

What it is not

It is not the live unlock table for the rail (that is play beats).

It is not specimen XP — individuals grow on their own track too.

What you do (first time)

  1. Finish a lawn match or other play that pays experience.
  2. Find Dave’s level on the HUD or Chronicle.
  3. If you have aptitude points, open builds and spend at least one.

Stop when “no cap” is clear. Next: type and specimen XP.

Common mix-ups

Is there a max Dave level?
No designed cap. Endless grind is the rule.
Does a new world reset Dave?
You keep who you are across worlds — see New world.
Why didn’t Dave 10 open Relics?
Dave chapters are Vision. Relics open on the live item beat.
Is Dave’s level the same as a demon’s level?
No. Demons have specimen XP; Dave is the summoner line.
