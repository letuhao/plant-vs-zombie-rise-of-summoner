Sanctum

The Sanctum is your home hall — the place you are when no lawn, battle, or map run is in progress, and where every run returns.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Stage
Where you are playing right now. The Sanctum is a stage — a place to *be*, not a list of websites to click through.
Layer
A menu that opens over the stage (Creatures, Fusion, Expeditions…). Closing it returns you to the same hall.
Rail
The shared set of layer hotkeys and entries. Same keys on every stage. Detail: layer rail.
Home
Not “main menu.” When nothing else is active, you stand in the hall with creatures, travel, and a sense of what is next.
  • The hall and rail are open in this build; some layers behind the rail are still thin. The guided first-run naming ritual is WIP — see naming ritual.
  • You arrive here after title / save select.

What it is

A summoner’s hall: creatures on display, a way to travel, and a focus on what to do next.

When a lawn, battle, delve, or siege ends, you come back here.

What you do here

  1. Read the hall

    Soul balance and summoner level sit on the HUD. Time in the Sanctum does not push you.

  2. Open layers

    Creatures, Fusion, Pacts, Expeditions, and the rest open over the hall without leaving it.

  3. Travel

    Go to the world map (M or the map table) or start a lawn run when a match is ready.

  4. Collect

    Take expedition returns that finished while you were away.

Expedition timers are wall-clock remaining — not a ticking panic meter in the hall.

Layers vs travel

Layers stack over the Sanctum and close back to the same place.

Travel replaces the stage when you leave for Lawn or World — that is a real departure, then you return home afterward.

Action What happens
Press C / F / E / … A layer opens over the hall
Esc (empty stack) System settings open
Travel (M / map table) You leave for the world map stage

First session note

Vision for a brand-new save: the hall starts almost empty; you bind and name your first creature in place.

Until that ritual ships, you still land in the Sanctum and open the rail yourself.

What you do (first time)

  1. Finish save select and land in the hall.
  2. Look around without opening everything — note HUD stocks and any “what next” cue.
  3. Open one layer (Creatures is a safe first), then close it and confirm you are still in the Sanctum.

Stop when the hall feels like home, not a settings dump. Next: layer rail and unlock beats.

Common mix-ups

Is the Sanctum just the main menu?
No. It is a home stage. Layers open over it; you stay in the hall.
Do I lose my place when I open Creatures?
No. Closing the layer returns you to the same Sanctum.
Why are some rail entries locked?
They unlock as you play. Locked entries say what opens them — see unlock beats.
Does standing here advance expeditions?
Expeditions run on wall-clock whether you watch or not. The hall does not add pressure.
Where is the naming ritual?
Not finished yet. You can still play from the Sanctum; see naming ritual.
