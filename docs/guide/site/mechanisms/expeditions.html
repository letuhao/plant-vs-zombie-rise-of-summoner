Expeditions

Expeditions are the idle RPG loop — pick spare demons, dispatch them, wait on a real-world timer, then collect.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Expedition
An idle squad mission you start from the Expeditions layer (E). Not a lawn match and not a delve.
Dispatch
Send demons who are not on a live task. The timer starts when you confirm.
Collect
Claim the return when ready. Nothing expires if you forget — the report waits.
Stamina gate
There is none. This build has no play-session stamina wall on expeditions.
  • Expeditions are a sibling of the Delve, not a prototype to replace. Auto-resolve stays; interactive battles you play yourself are WIP elsewhere.
  • Quest- and world-event-starting ticks stay Vision — see expedition event ticks.

What it is

Monster Hunter–style idle play that stays forever. Playable with the lawn game closed once the layer is open.

You pick demons, choose a duration tier, wait, and read the return report.

The Expeditions layer opens from the Sanctum rail (E) once you have a bound demon to field.

How it works

Four duration tiers are built for real schedules:

  pick spare demons  ->  dispatch  ->  wait  ->  collect
                                         |
                                         +->  souls / XP / materials / wild join?
  1. While they are out

    A battle chain interleaved with quiet stretches, found souls, meeting a wild demon, or injuries. Outcome is sealed at dispatch by a recorded seed and revealed at collection.

  2. What you bring home

    Souls and summoner XP, specimen XP for demons who fought, a chance a wave demon joins the roster, and fusion materials (essence). Materials feed actors and empire — never loam.

Tier Length
Short 30 minutes
Medium 4 hours
Long 8 hours
Overnight 20 hours

Where you find it

From the Sanctum

Expeditions sit on the layer rail.

  1. Open Expeditions (E) when the rail shows it.
  2. Pick demons who are not on a live task.
  3. Choose a duration tier, dispatch, then collect when the timer finishes (or recall early).

Start short. Grow into overnight runs when the roster can carry them.

What you do (first time)

  1. Bind at least one demon so Expeditions can open.
  2. Open Expeditions (E), pick a short tier and a spare squad, and dispatch.
  3. Collect when ready and read the return report once.

Stop when dispatch → wait → collect is clear. Next: slots and recall.

Common mix-ups

Do I need the lawn game open?
No. Once unlocked, expeditions run from the control room with the lawn closed.
Is this a delve?
No. Delves are a separate Vision stage. Expeditions stay the async idle loop.
Is there a stamina cost?
No. There is no stamina gate on expeditions.
Do rewards expire if I forget to collect?
No. Collect when you are ready.
Can expeditions start quests today?
Full quest and world-event ticks are Vision — see expedition event ticks.
