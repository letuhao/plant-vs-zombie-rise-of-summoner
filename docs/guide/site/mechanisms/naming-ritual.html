What it will feel like

A nearly empty hall on purpose.

You bind a first creature, give it a name, and get one clear next step — not a tour of every menu.

What you do today

Create a summoner at the door.

Enter the Sanctum and open Creatures when you are ready.

Treat any early naming or binding UI as temporary until this ritual owns the first session.

Do not wait for the ritual to play — the hall and rail are already open.

Why it matters

Without a naming beat, the first screen becomes a list of systems.

With it, the war starts as “this demon is yours,” then the rail opens around that story.

What you do while it is WIP

  1. Finish save select and enter the Sanctum.
  2. Open Creatures and meet whatever roster the build gives you.
  3. Ignore missing “guided empty hall” moments — they are this page’s unfinished work.

Stop when you know the ritual is coming, not broken. Home: Sanctum. Specimens: persistent specimens.
