Vision Idle expeditions · quests and world events

Expedition quest and event ticks

Vision: idle expedition ticks that open quests and world events — a story clock, not only loot. Not finished / not playable yet as that handoff.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Vision
Locked design. Full quest and world-event handoff from expedition ticks is not something you can rely on in the current loop.
Tick
A beat while an expedition is out — quiet stretches, finds, meetings, injuries today; quest/event starts later.
Quest handoff
A tick that opens a tracked quest (or world event), not only a material line in the return report.
Story clock
The world pushing narrative beats — opposite of a pure loot clock.
  • Dispatch → wait → collect already ships. Thin in-run expedition event flavour can appear without this Vision handoff.
  • Discoveries that open domains for the Delve hang on the same Vision story.

Not finished / not playable yet

Do not expect every expedition return to open a quest log entry or a world raid.

Read this page for the intended story clock — play loot and roster outcomes on expeditions today.

What it is (design)

While a squad is out, ticks can do more than pay materials.

Vision: some ticks start quests, surface world events, or discover doors into domains for the Delve.

  expedition running  ->  tick
                           |
                           +->  loot / injury / wild meet (today)
                           +->  quest / world event / domain door (Vision)

Where you would find it

When it ships

The fantasy hangs on Expeditions plus the quest layer.

  1. Dispatch as you do now.
  2. When a tick opens a quest or event, it would surface in the quest log / world events surfaces.
  3. Until then, treat missing quest prompts as Vision — not a broken expedition.

Full handoff is Vision even if thin expedition event ticks exist in reports.

What you do with this page

  1. Keep using dispatch → wait → collect for loot, XP, and wild joins.
  2. Do not wait on expedition returns for a finished quest log.
  3. When you want the story-clock fantasy, read quest log and world events as Vision siblings.

Stop when Vision ticks ≠ live payouts. Shipped loop: expeditions.

Common mix-ups

Why didn’t my overnight run open a quest?
Quest and world-event ticks are Vision. Absence is expected.
Are quiet stretches and injuries Vision too?
No. Those in-run tick flavours belong to the shipped expedition chain; the quest/event handoff is the Vision piece.
Will this replace expeditions?
No. Expeditions stay idle forever. Ticks add story, they do not delete the loop.
Is this the same as the quest log?
Related. Ticks would feed the log; the log itself is Vision — see quest log.
