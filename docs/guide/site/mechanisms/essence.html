Shipped Stocks and economy · summon and fusion

Essence

Essence is element-matched material you stockpile for fusion (and for some buildings later) — fire essence is not the same as ice essence.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Essence
A material stock in your save, split by element. Not souls, not loam, not a demon’s health.
Element-matched
A fusion that wants fire essence will not take ice essence instead. The colour (element) is the point of the bottleneck.
Bank
Essence stays with your summoner across worlds — same idea as souls. Loam does not.
Fusion
Merging specimens in the Fusion lab (F). That is the main place you spend essence today.
  • Essence is one of three stocks. The others are souls and loam. They do not convert into each other.
  • You may also see shards as fusion materials. Shards are rarity-matched fodder; essence is the element gate. This page is about essence.

What it is

Six colours of stock — fire, ice, air, earth, light, dark — matching the combat elements.

You earn them by playing. You spend them when a fusion (or later a building) asks for a specific colour.

Essence banks across worlds — see New world.

Why it exists

If every merge cost the same generic currency, element would stop mattering the moment you left combat.

Essence keeps the ring honest in the workshop: the result’s element asks for matching material, so farming and planning stay elemental.

How it works

Think of faucets and sinks — same shape as souls, different wallet:

  play / expeditions  ->  essence (by element)
                              |
                              +->  Fusion lab (main sink)
                              +->  buildings (when they ask)
  1. Faucets

    Lawn play, expedition returns, and map work can pay essence (and related materials).

  2. Sinks

    Specimen fusion spends the colour the recipe asks for. Some sector buildings later also ask for essence.

Where you find it

In the control room

Materials show up where fusion and expeditions care about them.

  1. Open Fusion (F) and read the cost panel — have / need for the essence colour.
  2. Open Expeditions and check materials after a return — drops include essences.
  3. On the HUD stock strip, essence sits beside souls as a stock you can bank.

Exact drop amounts change with balance. Trust the have/need numbers on screen.

What you do (first time)

  1. Finish a lawn match or collect an expedition that can pay materials.
  2. Open Fusion (F) with a merge in mind.
  3. Read the cost panel: if essence is short, note the colour you need — then farm that colour, not a different one.

Stop here when you can tell fire essence from ice essence. Next: how merges work in specimen fusion.

Common mix-ups

Can I convert fire essence into ice?
No. Colours do not swap. That bottleneck is intentional.
Is essence the same as souls?
No. Souls buy summons and keep pacts. Essence feeds fusion’s element gate. See souls.
Does essence fade if I neglect the map?
No. Only loam fades with neglected ground. Essence banks.
Do I need essence to pull at the altar?
No. The altar spends souls. Essence shows up when you fuse.
Are shards the same thing?
No. Shards are another fusion material (rarity fodder). Essence is the element-matched stock.
