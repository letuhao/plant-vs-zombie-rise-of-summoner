Summoning altar

The altar is where you spend souls to pull new demons into the roster — pity is shown so you always know how close the next strong pull is.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Altar
The summon surface in the control room (Summon / Demons). Not a lawn plant, not a world-map building.
Pull
One spend of souls that adds a specimen. Costs are on screen — this guide does not invent numbers.
Pity
Progress toward a stronger result. Shown on the altar so the bank does not feel like a black box.
Gacha
Random pulls for new demons. One intake path — wild joins and capture are others.
  • Altar spends souls, not essence.
  • New specimens land on the roster.

What it is

A soul sink that grows the roster.

You watch pity climb so strong pulls are readable, not hidden.

How a pull feels

  play  ->  souls  ->  altar pull  ->  new specimen
  1. Earn souls from play.
  2. Open the altar when you can afford a pull.
  3. Confirm the spend — souls leave the bank with no refund.
  4. Read the new specimen on Creatures.

What the altar is not

It is not the only way to get demons.

It is not Fusion (F) — fusion merges specimens you already have.

What you do (first time)

  1. Check souls on the HUD after a lawn match or other play.
  2. Open Summon / Demons and read the pity and cost on screen.
  3. Pull once when you can afford it, then open Creatures (C) to meet the new specimen.

Stop when altar ≠ fusion is clear. Bind next: pacts and loyalty.

Common mix-ups

Do I spend essence here?
No. Essence is for fusion’s element gate. The altar spends souls.
Can I refund a bad pull?
No. Confirmed spends leave the bank.
Is pity secret?
No. It is shown on the altar on purpose.
Must I pull forever to grow?
No. Wild joins and other intakes exist — gacha is one path.
