Souls

Souls are the currency you earn by playing and spend to call new demons and keep them bound.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Soul
A currency number in your save. Not a demon, not a life bar, not something you lose on death.
Spend
Souls leave your bank the moment you confirm at the altar. There is no refund.
Bank
One shared pool for the whole save. It is not per demon and not per world.
Earn
Play pays. Waiting does not — there is no idle soul trickle.
  • Souls are one of three stocks. The others are essence and loam, and they do not convert into each other.

What it is

One bank, shown in the HUD wherever you are.

You watch it climb while you play and drop when you summon.

Souls bank across worlds — see New world.

How it works

Three things touch the number:

  play  ->  souls  ->  altar  ->  demons
                       +->  pacts
  1. Faucets

    Lawn matches, expedition returns, and map work pay souls.

  2. Sinks

    Altar pulls and pact upkeep spend them.

Where What you get
Lawn match Souls on a win, scaled by what you fought
Expedition Souls in the return report

Where you find it

In the HUD

The stock strip is always on screen.

  1. Look at the top strip.
  2. Souls sit beside essence.

It updates without a refresh.

What you do (first time)

  1. Play a lawn match to the end.
  2. Open the altar.
  3. Spend once, and read what the pull cost.

Stop here. Pacts come next.

Common mix-ups

Do I lose souls when a demon dies?
No. Souls are a bank, not a health pool.
Can I buy souls?
No. There is no store — this build is local and has no payments.
Do souls decay?
No. Only loam fades if you neglect ground.
Is this the same as essence?
No. See essence.
