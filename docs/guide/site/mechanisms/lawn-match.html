Lawn match

A lawn match is a live board match (Plants vs. Zombies: Fusion on your machine) — the first core loop, not the whole war.

What it is

Legal Plants vs. Zombies: Fusion on your machine, with the control room watching.

You play the board. Rise of Summoner records what happened and can send roster demons back into the fight.

Point the launcher at a legal install. Uninstalling the plugin is deleting a folder.

What a run leaves behind

A normal lawn level ends when the wave ends. Here it leaves something:

  legal match  ->  play board  ->  souls / XP / almanac / levels
                      |
                      +->  deploy roster demons (when bound)
  1. Souls

    Kills and victories feed the soul ledger.

  2. Almanac and types

    Plants and zombies you meet feed type progression and the almanac.

  3. Specimen levels

    Specimens gain levels from work they actually did.

  4. Deployed demons

    Bound demons you field fight with the traits and element you gave them.

Where you find it

First session shape

  1. Start a legal lawn-game match from your install.
  2. Open the control room and go to the Lawn stage while it runs.
  3. Play the match; when it ends, check souls and roster progress in the Sanctum.

Closing a menu over the lawn does not destroy the board — you stay on the stage.

What you do (first time)

  1. Launch a legal match.
  2. Open Lawn in the control room and confirm the live mirror is moving.
  3. Finish a wave and look for souls / almanac / specimen progress in your save.

Read the board under pressure: live mirror and unit HUD. Field roster demons: bound demon deploy.

Common mix-ups

Is the lawn the whole game?
No. It is the first core loop. Expeditions, map, and later places matter too.
Do I lose RPG progress if I close the lawn game?
Unlocked web features stay playable with the lawn game closed. RPG data lives beside the server.
Does Rise of Summoner patch the game binary?
No. It does not download, patch, or rewrite the binary.
Are cheats part of this guide?
Sandbox cheats exist for builders, off by default — not part of normal play guidance.
Is blessing required to earn souls?
No. Souls already pay from play. Blessing (WIP) is meant to boost later idle — see lawn blessing.
