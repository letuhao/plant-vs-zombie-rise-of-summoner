Specimen fusion

Fusion merges specimens into stronger forms and lets you discover recipes — the rail key F is this lab, not the host pack’s product name.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Fusion (menu)
Specimen merge in Rise of Summoner. Separate from the lawn pack name Plants vs. Zombies: Fusion.
Recipe
A known merge path you discover by experimenting — not a wiki checklist you must memorize first.
Star merge
A merge that preserves identity and pushes power on that line.
Promotion
Raising rarity once, with a cost — a step up the display ladder, not a free rename.
  • Fusion spends essence (element-matched) and often shards — read the have/need panel.
  • Ingredients are specimens; the altar is a different intake.

What you can do

  1. Star merges

    Keep identity, push power.

  2. Recipes

    Discover by trying merges; silhouettes hint once you have met the species.

  3. Trait inheritance

    Builds come from merges — not only bigger numbers.

  4. Promotion

    Raise rarity once when the lab offers it, at a cost.

How a merge feels

  specimens + essence (+ shards)  ->  Fusion lab  ->  stronger specimen / discovery
  1. Open Fusion (F).
  2. Pick a base and sacrifices (locked or away-on-expedition specimens stay unavailable).
  3. Read souls / shards / essence have-vs-need.
  4. Confirm only when the colours and counts line up.

Exact costs are on screen and in balance data — this page does not invent numbers.

Why element essence matters

The result’s element asks for matching essence so the combat ring still matters in the workshop.

Fire essence will not stand in for ice — see essence.

What you do (first time)

  1. Unlock Fusion (you need a demon you can actually fuse).
  2. Open F and pick a cheap experiment you can afford.
  3. Watch discovery or power change, then check the specimen on Creatures.

Stop when Fusion ≠ host-pack name is automatic. Optional WIP: patron.

Common mix-ups

Is this the lawn game’s fusion minigame?
No. This is the RPG specimen lab on key F.
Do I need a wiki to start?
No. Experiment — recipes reveal as you succeed.
Can I fuse with any essence colour?
No. Match the element the cost panel asks for.
Why is a specimen greyed out?
It may be locked or away (for example on an expedition).
