Commanders

Choose who leads the next lawn run. The choice persists; a match snapshot freezes the leader when the board starts — you do not mid-wave swap the story.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Commander
Who leads the next lawn — open with K. Dave is the default face until you have more names.
Snapshot
When the match starts, the leader is frozen for that run.
WIP
Commander pick and aptitude spend exist in thin form; auras and fuller fantasy still catch up.
Patron
Different system — element colour of the army. Not the same as commander.
  • Aura presence from that leader is commander auras (WIP).
  • Aptitude spend at commander scope reaches the side — see aptitudes.

How you use it

  1. Open Commanders (K).
  2. Set the leader for the next lawn.
  3. Start the match — the snapshot locks that leader for the run.

What you do not do

You do not mid-wave swap the story.

You do not treat commander as a class pick — free-build aptitudes still rule.

What you do (first time)

  1. Press K from the Sanctum.
  2. Confirm who is set as default lawn commander.
  3. Play a lawn match without trying to change leader mid-board.

Aura layer: commander auras.

Common mix-ups

Can I swap commanders mid-wave?
No. Snapshot at match start.
Is Dave required forever?
Default face until you have other commander names to put there.
Same as patron?
No.
Why is this WIP if K opens?
Thin pick/spend can ship while auras and depth stay WIP.
