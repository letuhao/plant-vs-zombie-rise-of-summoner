Statuses

Statuses are timed conditions — butter, freeze, chill, poison, hypno, wither, contagions, and more — that actually resolve in combat.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Status
A timed condition on a unit. Not a permanent class, not a stock, not an element on the ring.
Apply / cleanse
Statuses go on and come off through play. You do not buy them from a shop tab.
Catalog vs reach
The catalog is real; not every status is common in every mode yet. Missing one in a mode is reachability, not a fake row.
Contagion
Statuses that spread or mark (blight, rot, spark, and kin) — still statuses, not a second combat system.
Delve nerve ladder
Unsettled → Shaken → Afflicted is Vision for delve pressure — not something every lawn already runs.
  • You read icons on the lawn unit HUD under pressure.
  • How hard a status sticks ties to resistance.

What it is

Conditions with a clock. They change what a unit can do or how the next hits feel.

Familiar lawn wraps sit beside overlay pressure and contagion — one status language.

Families you will see

Names from the combat pillar — not an exhaustive move list:

  1. Crowd control and lawn wraps

    Butter, Freeze, Chill, Poison, Hypno, Ember, Jala, Kelp.

  2. Extra pressure

    Wither, Bond, Rally, Leech, Expose, Command, Shatter, Charm Pulse.

  3. Contagion

    Blight, Rot, Spark, Pact Mark, Spore.

  4. Vision (delve nerve)

    Unsettled → Shaken → Afflicted — locked as design for delve, not everyday lawn soup.

Exact durations and stacks are balance surface. Trust icons and tooltips in the build you have.

Where you find it

On the lawn today

  1. Open Lawn during a match.
  2. Read status chips on the unit HUD above units.
  3. Apply and cleanse through ordinary play — plants, zombies, skills when they land.

Battle-stage UI for the same icons is still catching up with interactive battles.

What you do (first time)

  1. Run a lawn match with Lawn open in the control room.
  2. Point at one status icon and name it (butter, freeze, chill, poison — whatever appears).
  3. Watch whether it sticks, fades, or fails — that is apply, cleanse, and resist in one glance.

Element matchups: six elements and omni. Named combo recipes on top stay Vision — named reactions.

Common mix-ups

Is a status the same as an element?
No. Elements are types on the ring. Statuses are timed conditions.
Why don’t I see every name from the list?
The catalog is real; reachability grows with content. Absence in a mode is not a broken row.
Do statuses only exist on the lawn?
No. They are combat language everywhere — lawn reach is just the first place you read them.
Is Unsettled / Shaken / Afflicted live everywhere?
That ladder is Vision for delve nerve. Do not expect it as daily lawn soup yet.
Can I buy statuses with souls?
No. They apply through play, skills, and content — not a stock spend.
