Shipped Session and shell · how you play

Unlock ladder (play beats)

Layers on the rail unlock as you play — locked entries stay visible and tell you what opens them, instead of vanishing or sitting as dead grey weight.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Beat
A play milestone (first item, first demon you can fuse, first bound demon…). Not a music beat, not Dave’s level number.
Unlock ladder
The live order those beats open layers. Today this ladder is beat-based, not level-gated.
Locked
You can see the layer on the rail; you cannot use it yet. The UI should say what unlocks it.
Dave-level chapters
A Vision drip of features by Dave’s level. Not what gates the live rail today — see unlock chapters.

Live unlock table

What opens the rail in this build:

Layer Unlocks when
Creatures, Commanders Session start
Relics You hold your first item
Fusion You have a demon you can fuse
Pacts A contract is first offered
Expeditions You have a bound demon to field
Almanac, Chronicle After your first run

Delve travel (Vision) unlocks when you have found a domain — not on this live table yet.

Why beats, not a silent menu

Invisible features teach nothing. Greyed icons with no reason teach frustration.

Beats keep the rail honest: you always know the next verb that will open a layer.

What this is not

It is not the Vision Dave-level chapter schedule (Dave 10 items, Dave 20 expeditions, and so on).

That chapter map will drip complexity by level later. Until then, play beats rule the rail you have now.

What you do (first time)

  1. Open the rail and find a locked layer.
  2. Read the unlock hint on that entry.
  3. Play until that beat happens, then open the layer once to feel it land.

Stop when locked entries feel like promises, not bugs. Optional Vision contrast: unlock chapters.

Common mix-ups

Why is Fusion locked when I have demons?
You need a demon the lab can actually fuse. Owning a roster is not always enough.
I hit Dave level 10 — where are Relics?
Dave chapters are Vision. Relics open when you hold your first item (this page’s table).
Did the game hide Almanac?
No. It unlocks after your first run. Until then it should still show as locked with a reason.
Is this the same as naming ritual?
No. Naming ritual is a first-session WIP beat in the hall — see naming ritual.
Will beats go away when chapters ship?
Chapters are meant to drip complexity by Dave’s level. The product still wants clear unlock messaging either way.
