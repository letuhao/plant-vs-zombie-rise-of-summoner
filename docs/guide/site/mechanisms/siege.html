Siege

Vision: when the war hits the Seat — yours or theirs — the fight becomes a siege board: structures, lanes of approach, occupy to possess. Not finished / not playable yet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Vision
Not playable yet. You cannot open a siege stage in the current loop.
Siege
A tactical board for Seat defence or assault — buildings as actors, occupy to possess.
Seat
The capital fight focus — homeworld defence or Zomboss fortress assault.
Stage
You travel there to act; on resolve you return to the Sanctum — like delve, unlike a layer.
  • Siege is the defend beat of farm / hunt / defend made into a stage.
  • Map pressure from Zomboss already ships; this page is the Seat board fantasy on top.

Not finished / not playable yet

You cannot enter a siege today. Sanctum, Lawn, World, and expeditions carry play.

Outcome will feed back to the rift when the stage ships.

What it is (design)

Defend the homeworld. Assault Zomboss’s fortress when you find it.

The fight is not only a graph node — structures matter, lanes of approach matter, possession of buildings matters.

  war hits Seat  ->  siege board
                      |
                      +->  defend homeworld
                      +->  assault fortress
                      v
                 outcome -> rift

What it needs

Interactive battles you play yourself are WIP shared muscle.

Farm / hunt / defend names why the Seat matters even before the board exists.

What you do with this page

  1. Defend the homeworld on the shipped map — losing it still ends the world.
  2. Do not hunt for a siege board button yet — it is Vision.
  3. When sieges ship, expect a return to the Sanctum after resolve.

Stop when siege ≠ any map contact. Related: farm / hunt / defend.

Common mix-ups

Can I siege today?
No. Not finished / not playable yet.
Is every map fight a siege?
No. Siege is the Seat board. Ordinary contacts hang on map / battle handoff.
Does Zomboss pressure require siege?
No. He already acts on the shipped map. Siege is the capital-stage fantasy.
Is siege a delve?
No. Delve is the dungeon crawl; siege is Seat defence/assault.
