Sector buildings

WIP: raise economy on ground you hold — wells, waystations, granaries, and kin — paid in loam and sometimes essence. Not finished / not fully playable yet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

WIP
Designed and partly built. Do not expect a finished construction loop on every held sector today.
Sector building
A structure on held ground that raises economy or support — not a lawn plant card.
Loam cost
Buildings spend world stock. Exact amounts are balance data — trust the UI when it ships.
Farm beat
This is the empire farm verb — yield and upkeep on ground you keep.
  • Named examples in the fiction include wells, waystations, granaries, deep roots, and soul conduits — not a closed final list.
  • The finished place you stand while building is world stage (Vision as a finished place).

What you do today

Play the shipped thin map: claim, hold, watch loam.

Treat missing construction panels as unfinished work, not a broken save.

Where it will live

Empire surface

When it ships, buildings raise from the empire / sector surface on ground you hold.

  1. Hold a sector with a working loam story.
  2. Open the sector / empire construction UI when it appears.
  3. Spend loam (and essence when asked) — read on-screen costs, never a remembered constant.

Not finished yet. Fantasy only until the loop hardens.

What you do while it is WIP

  1. Keep claiming and holding ground so farm has somewhere to land.
  2. Watch loam — buildings will spend it, not souls.
  3. When construction UI appears, follow have/need on screen and ignore remembered numbers.

Stop when building ≠ altar pull. Related: loam, world stage.

Common mix-ups

Can I build everywhere today?
No. Sector buildings are WIP — absence is expected.
Do buildings cost souls?
Design spends loam, sometimes essence — not souls as the building wallet.
Does loam bank with the building into the next world?
No. Loam and that map’s holdings stay behind.
Is this the world stage?
Buildings hang on the farm/empire beat. The finished stage place is a separate Vision page.
