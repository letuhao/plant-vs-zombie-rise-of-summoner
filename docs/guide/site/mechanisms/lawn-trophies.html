Lawn trophies

WIP: cosmetic prestige from lawn play — never stats, never a repeatable faucet. Not finished yet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

WIP
Not finished. You should not expect a full trophy case as a live grind yet.
Trophy
Display prestige from lawn play. Not gear, not a soul sink, not a combat buff.
Never stats
Design rule: trophies do not raise power.
Never a faucet
Design rule: trophies are not a repeatable currency printer.
Chronicle
Your history of runs and progression — related memory, not the same as a trophy shelf. See chronicle.
  • Blessing (soul boost on idle) and capture (roster intake) are different WIP lawn outcomes.
  • Play lawn for real faucets today — souls, XP, almanac — not for missing trophies.

Not finished yet

When it ships, earn trophies from play for display only.

Until then, treat a missing trophy shelf as unfinished work.

Design guardrails

Why the page exists before the UI:

  lawn play  ->  trophy (WIP, display)
            ->  souls / XP / almanac (shipped faucets)
  1. Cosmetic

    Show what you did on the lawn.

  2. No power

    Never stats — prestige does not win fights.

  3. No faucet

    Never a repeatable currency printer disguised as medals.

What you do today

Chase shipped lawn outcomes: souls, levels, almanac meets, deploy.

Use chronicle for history that already records runs.

What you do while it is WIP

  1. Play lawn for souls and roster progress now.
  2. When trophies appear, treat them as display — do not theorycraft power from them.
  3. If a build ever ties power to trophies, that violates the design — flag it.

Parent: lawn match. Memory sibling: chronicle.

Common mix-ups

Where do I equip trophies for damage?
Nowhere. Design forbids stats on trophies.
Can I farm trophies for souls?
No. They must never be a repeatable faucet.
Is this the same as blessing?
No. Blessing is temporary idle soul pressure. Trophies are cosmetic.
Why is there no trophy UI?
WIP — not finished.
Does chronicle replace trophies?
Chronicle is history. Trophies are prestige display when they ship — related memory, different face.
