The Delve

Vision: a party crawl through seeded rooms toward a boss — haul out or fail trying. Not finished / not playable yet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Vision
Locked fiction. You cannot enter a delve in the current loop.
Delve
A stage you travel to — room graph, pack, extract — not an expedition and not a lawn match.
Domain
A named place you find in the world, then enter from the Sanctum.
Stage vs layer
Delve is a stage (you go there to act). Almanac is a layer (opens over where you already are).
  • Expeditions stay the idle sibling — delves do not replace them.
  • Interactive battles you play yourself are WIP shared with delves; formations and oaths are Vision depth.

What it is (design)

You find domains out in the world (expedition discoveries are part of that story). From the Sanctum you pick a found domain and enter.

Inside: a room graph — fights, elites, caches, shrines, merchants, traps, wild talk. You manage a pack. You extract with a haul — or you wipe.

  find domain  ->  Sanctum enter  ->  room graph
                                      |
                                      +->  extract with haul
                                      +->  wipe / fail

Unlock (when it ships): first domain found on an expedition.

Common mix-ups

Can I enter a delve today?
No. Not finished / not playable yet.
Is this just a long expedition?
No. Expeditions auto-resolve idle. Delves are a presence stage with rooms and extract.
Where do I start one?
Design: find a domain, then enter from the Sanctum — when it ships.
Do delves replace expeditions?
No. They are siblings.
