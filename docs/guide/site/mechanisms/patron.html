What it will be

Pick one demon from the roster as patron.

Its element becomes the army’s tint; changing that choice costs souls.

In this build

The patron idea exists in simulation.

The live lawn aura and full player flow are still WIP — do not expect a finished patron button on every stage.

Why it matters later

Without a patron, element identity is only on individual specimens.

With one, the side carries a readable elemental story into lawn and battles.

What you do while it is WIP

  1. Raise specimens and learn elements on the roster.
  2. Treat any partial patron UI as unfinished.
  3. Use commanders and fusion as they exist today without waiting on patron.

Stop when patron ≠ commander is clear. Other intakes: wild joins, capture.

Common mix-ups

Is patron the same as commander?
No. Patron colours element identity; commanders pick who leads a lawn run.
Can I have three patrons?
Design is one patron. Do not plan multi-patron loadouts.
Is my build broken without it?
No. WIP means unfinished, not required to open the game.
Does patron replace essence?
No. Essence remains fusion’s element material.
