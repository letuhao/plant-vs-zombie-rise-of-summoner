Pack, extract, oaths, domains

Vision: provision a pack, enter a found domain, extract with a haul or wipe — optional hard oaths on difficulty rungs. Not finished / not playable yet.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Vision
Not playable yet. These pieces exist as locked design for the Delve.
Pack
The party you take into the room graph — not an expedition squad timer slot by another name.
Extract
Leave with the haul — success path opposite a wipe.
Oath
Optional hard stakes / permadeath-style pressure on difficulty rungs — not a pact tribute payment.
  • Nerve and spirit pressure belong here — Unsettled → Shaken → Afflicted as the delve gets under your skin.
  • Wild talk can open recruit or capture paths inside the crawl.

Not finished / not playable yet

You cannot provision a delve pack or swear an oath on a live domain today.

Read the table so the words stay honest when the stage ships.

What it is (design)

  find domain  ->  provision pack  ->  enter
                                      |
                                      +->  extract (+ haul)
                                      +->  wipe (oaths may bite harder)
Piece What it means
Domains Named places you find, then enter from the Sanctum
Pack The party you take into the room graph
Extract Leave with the haul — or fail trying
Oaths / permadeath Optional hard stakes on difficulty rungs

How it hangs on failure

A failed extract is not only a dead run when failure branches ship — it can open different ground.

Formations are a second axis beside who is in the pack — see party formations.

What you do with this page

  1. Learn the four words: domain, pack, extract, oath.
  2. Do not map them onto expedition recall — different stage, different stakes.
  3. When delves ship, provision deliberately before you enter.

Stop when oath ≠ pact tribute. Parent stage: the Delve.

Common mix-ups

Is a pack an expedition slot?
No. Packs are for the Delve room graph. Expeditions stay idle dispatch.
Are oaths the same as pacts?
No. Pacts are roster loyalty. Oaths are optional hard delve stakes.
Can I extract today?
No. Not finished / not playable yet.
Does wipe always mean nothing happens?
Vision failure branches can open new content from loss — see failure branches.
