Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Almanac
The reference layer (A) for creature types, elements, statuses, recipes, and effect families as they become visible.
Dossier
A filed record of something you discovered — portrait, pedia text, costs, type info.
Discovery state
An empty slot means you have not met it yet — not a broken link.
Developer dump
Raw export pages are not the almanac. If it looks like a dump, it is not for players.
  • Some dossiers are still thin; presentation polish continues.

What it is

See something in the game and the RPG files it.

You end up with a browsable set of dossiers and fusion recipes you uncovered — a record of your run through the war.

How it works

Reference material, open over any stage:

  meet in play  ->  filed dossier / recipe
                       |
                       v
                 Almanac layer (A)
  1. Types

    Creature types you have discovered.

  2. Ring and statuses

    Elements and statuses as they become player-visible.

  3. Recipes

    Fusion recipes you uncover sit beside dossiers.

Codex milestones for demons reward exploration. Web-only players can still claim completion milestones without every exclusive lawn capture.

Where you find it

Almanac layer (A)

Open over Sanctum or any stage when the rail allows.

  1. Meet something in play (lawn, expedition, map — wherever discovery fires).
  2. Open Almanac (A).
  3. Browse dossiers and recipes; empty slots are unmet, not errors.

Older “types / recipes / runs” routes redirect here as the Sanctum model finishes replacing old audit screens.

What you do (first time)

  1. Play until you have met at least one new type or recipe.
  2. Open Almanac (A) and find that dossier.
  3. Leave one empty slot alone — that is discovery state working.

Stop when almanac ≠ wiki download. Sibling ledger: chronicle.

Common mix-ups

Why are some slots empty?
You have not discovered them yet. Empty means unmet.
Is this Chronicle?
No. Chronicle is history and XP ledgers (H).
Can web-only play finish milestones?
Design allows completion milestones without every exclusive lawn capture.
Is a raw data dump the almanac?
No. Developer dumps are not for players.
