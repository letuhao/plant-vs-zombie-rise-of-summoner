Loam and the Fracture

Loam is the pressure of ground you hold on the rift map — neglect it and the Fracture fades that holding away. It never banks into the next world.

Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

Loam
A world stock tied to sectors you hold. It is position and upkeep, not a wallet you carry forever.
The Fracture
The force that unmakes ground you do not keep real. Neglect → fade → loss. Not a combat status.
Bank
What souls and essence do across worlds. Loam does not bank — when a world ends, loam and that map’s holdings stay behind.
Sector
A place on the rift map you can claim, march through, or lose. Loam stories live on sectors.
  • Loam is one of three stocks. The others are souls and essence. They do not convert into each other.
  • The surface is playable but thin in this build — deeper empire verbs and buildings are still catching up.

What it is

Every sector carries a loam story: fertile ground supports an expanding empire; barren ground drains one.

Territory you neglect fades, then is lost to the Fracture. Structures die with the fade; some natural rootbeds remain as places you can found on again.

Ending a world keeps your roster, souls, and essence — not loam. See New world.

Why it exists

Without a stock that dies with the map, empire play collapses into “farm forever, then leave.”

Loam makes holding ground a real choice: expand, maintain, or lose what you claimed.

How it works

Think in map verbs, not shop tabs:

  claim sector  ->  loam pressure on that ground
       |
       +->  maintain  ->  holding stays
       +->  neglect   ->  fade  ->  Fracture takes it
  1. Hold

    Claimed sectors stay real while their loam story supports them.

  2. Neglect

    Fade is gradual and visible — you get a chance to notice before the Fracture takes the holding.

  3. Spend

    Buildings and position cost loam (sometimes with essence). That is the farm beat of empire.

Stock Scope
Loam This world only — never banks
Souls Player bank across worlds
Essence Player bank across worlds (by element)

Where you find it

On the rift map

Open World travel when you are ready to march.

  1. Travel to the world map from Sanctum.
  2. Read loam on sectors you hold or want.
  3. Watch for fade on neglected ground before it is gone.

Sector buildings that spend loam are still WIP as a full farm loop — see sector buildings.

What you do (first time)

  1. Open the world map when travel is available.
  2. Claim or inspect a sector and notice its loam story.
  3. Decide once: hold it (come back) or leave it — neglect is a choice with a cost.

Stop when you can say why loam is not a second soul bank. Next: farm / hunt / defend and the world map.

Common mix-ups

Can I save loam for the next world?
No. Loam and that map’s holdings stay behind. Roster, souls, and essence bank.
Is loam the same as souls?
No. Souls buy roster power. Loam buys and keeps ground. See souls.
Does essence fade with the Fracture?
No. Only loam and neglected holdings. Essence banks — see essence.
If ground fades, is my whole save wiped?
No. You lose that holding’s position on the map. Your summoner bank (roster, souls, essence) remains.
Is the Fracture a boss I fight?
Not as a single boss fight. It is the rule that unanchored ground stops being real.
