## In one sentence

The **Sanctum** is your home hall — the place you are when no lawn, battle, or map run is in progress, and where every run returns.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | Where you are playing right now. The Sanctum is a stage — a place to *be*, not a list of websites to click through. |
| **Layer** | A menu that opens **over** the stage (Creatures, Fusion, Expeditions…). Closing it returns you to the same hall. |
| **Rail** | The shared set of layer hotkeys and entries. Same keys on every stage. Detail: [layer rail](layer-rail.md). |
| **Home** | Not “main menu.” When nothing else is active, you stand in the hall with creatures, travel, and a sense of what is next. |

**Also true:**

- The hall and rail are open in this build; some layers behind the rail are still thin. The guided first-run naming ritual is WIP — see [naming ritual](naming-ritual.md).
- You arrive here after [title / save select](save-select.md).

## What it is

A summoner’s hall: creatures on display, a way to travel, and a focus on what to do next.

When a lawn, battle, delve, or siege ends, you come back here.

---

## What you do here

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Read the hall** | Soul balance and summoner level sit on the HUD. Time in the Sanctum does not push you. |
| **Open layers** | Creatures, Fusion, Pacts, Expeditions, and the rest open over the hall without leaving it. |
| **Travel** | Go to the world map (`M` or the map table) or start a lawn run when a match is ready. |
| **Collect** | Take expedition returns that finished while you were away. |

> Expedition timers are wall-clock remaining — not a ticking panic meter in the hall.

---

## Layers vs travel

Layers stack over the Sanctum and close back to the same place.

Travel replaces the stage when you leave for Lawn or World — that is a real departure, then you return home afterward.

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| Press C / F / E / … | A layer opens over the hall |
| Esc (empty stack) | System settings open |
| Travel (M / map table) | You leave for the world map stage |

## First session note

Vision for a brand-new save: the hall starts almost empty; you bind and name your first creature in place.

Until that ritual ships, you still land in the Sanctum and open the rail yourself.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Finish [save select](save-select.md) and land in the hall.
2. Look around without opening everything — note HUD stocks and any “what next” cue.
3. Open one layer (Creatures is a safe first), then close it and confirm you are still in the Sanctum.

> Stop when the hall feels like home, not a settings dump. Next: [layer rail](layer-rail.md) and [unlock beats](unlock-beats.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Is the Sanctum just the main menu?**  
No. It is a home stage. Layers open over it; you stay in the hall.

**Do I lose my place when I open Creatures?**  
No. Closing the layer returns you to the same Sanctum.

**Why are some rail entries locked?**  
They unlock as you play. Locked entries say what opens them — see [unlock beats](unlock-beats.md).

**Does standing here advance expeditions?**  
Expeditions run on wall-clock whether you watch or not. The hall does not add pressure.

**Where is the naming ritual?**  
Not finished yet. You can still play from the Sanctum; see [naming ritual](naming-ritual.md).
