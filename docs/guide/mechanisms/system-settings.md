# Settings and keymap

**Status:** Shipped  
**Loop:** Session — how you play — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Sanctum](../sanctum.md)

---

## In one sentence

**System** is where you rebind keys, adjust display, and quit — open it with Esc when no layer is stacked on top.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **System** | The settings layer for Rise of Summoner’s control room — not the lawn game’s own options menu. |
| **Empty stack** | No Creatures/Fusion/… layer is open. Only then does Esc open System instead of closing a layer. |
| **Keymap** | Which keys open which rail verbs. It should match what the [layer rail](layer-rail.md) does. |
| **Quit** | Leaves the control-room session from System. On some browser-only paths Quit may be unavailable — close the tab or use the launcher. |

**Also true:**

- From the title screen, Settings jumps into System over Sanctum — same surface.
- Developer telemetry and cheat menus are not this page and are not player features.

---

## How you open it

1. Close any open layers until you are back on the bare stage.
2. Press Esc — System opens.
3. Or choose Settings on the title screen.

> If Esc only closes a panel, you still had a layer open — that is correct stack behavior.

---

## What you change here

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Keybinds** | Rebind rail and travel keys. Pressing ? should agree with the rail. |
| **Display** | How the control room looks on your machine. |
| **Quit** | Leave the session cleanly when the build supports it. |

---

## What stays elsewhere

Lawn-game graphics, volume, and packs options stay in the host game.

Installer trust and antivirus steps stay in the launcher / player runbook — not inside System.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. From Sanctum, press Esc with nothing else open.
2. Find the keymap and confirm it matches C / F / E / … on the rail.
3. Close System and confirm you return to the same stage.

> Stop when Esc feels predictable. Rail detail: [layer rail](layer-rail.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Esc closed Creatures instead of Settings — bug?**  
No. Esc closes the top layer first. Open System only on an empty stack.

**Where are lawn-game options?**  
In Plants vs. Zombies: Fusion itself. System is the RPG control room.

**Title Settings vs Esc System?**  
Same System surface — different doors in.

**Can I rebind lawn plant keys here?**  
No. This keymap is for Rise of Summoner layers and travel.
