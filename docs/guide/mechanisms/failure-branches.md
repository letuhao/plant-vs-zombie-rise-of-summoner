# Failure branches

**Status:** Vision  
**Loop:** Quests and events · Farm / hunt / defend · Delve — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Delves and sieges](../delves-and-sieges.md)  
**HTML guide:** [site/mechanisms/failure-branches.html](../site/mechanisms/failure-branches.html)

---

## In one sentence

**Vision:** a lost sector, failed extract, or failed defence can open **different ground** — not only a wipe. Consequence as content. Not finished / not playable yet.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Not playable yet. Loss today does not reliably open new questlines or domains by this rule. |
| **Failure branch** | Content that starts because you failed — bandits, a domain, a quest — not only “try again.” |
| **Wipe** | The dead-run end. Branches sit beside wipe, not instead of stakes. |
| **Different ground** | New map pressure or places opened by loss — shared with farm / hunt / defend and delve. |

**Also true:**

- Ending a world still follows keep-who-you-are / lose-where-you-were — branches are not a prestige wipe of the summoner.
- Named on both quests and territorial loops so it cannot hide.

---

## Not finished / not playable yet

Do not expect every lost sector or failed run to open a curated branch today.

The design promise is consequence as content when this Vision ships.

---

## What it is (design)

Loss can unlock paths:

```text
  fail (sector / extract / defence)
            |
            +->  wipe / loss stakes
            +->  branch content (Vision)
```

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lost sector** | Map failure opens bandits, pressure, or a new problem to solve. |
| **Failed extract** | Delve wipe can open a domain, quest, or other ground — not only emptiness. |
| **Failed defence** | Failed defence can open different ground as content — still beside real lose stakes (homeworld fall ends the world). |

> Win/lose of a world still matter — see [new world](new-world-prestige.md).

---

## Where it hangs

Quests and events name the story clock.

Farm / hunt / defend and the Delve are where the failures happen.

---

## What you do with this page

1. Play for keeps — branches do not mean failure is free.
2. Treat missing “new quest from wipe” prompts as Vision.
3. When branches ship, read them as content you unlocked the hard way.

> Stop when failure ≠ only a reset button. Related: [quest log](quest-log.md), [delve](delve.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**I wiped — where is my branch?**  
Failure branches are Vision. Absence is expected.

**Does failure wipe my souls?**  
No. World loss drops loam and holdings; roster/souls/essence bank — see [new world](new-world-prestige.md).

**Is this soft failure with no stakes?**  
No. Branches sit beside real loss — consequence as content, not a free retry.

**Only delves?**  
No. Lost sectors and failed defences are in scope too.
