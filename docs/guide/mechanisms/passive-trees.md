## In one sentence

**Vision:** spend souls into trees for identity and risk — many trees can be equal in expected value; concentrating buys reward and risk.

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Not in the current loop. You cannot open finished trees as a live system yet. |
| **Tree** | A learnable passive catalog you spend into — a face for the build, not a class pick. |
| **Concentrate** | Stacking one tree harder for bigger reward and bigger risk. |
| **Shared catalogs** | Trees are meant to be learnable and shared — not locked behind a secret wiki. |

**Also true:**

- Respec costs souls in the design.
- Live power today is aptitudes and Dave — see [aptitudes](aptitudes.md).

## Design intent

Trees give a build a face.

Equal EV across many trees keeps choice honest; concentration is the risk knob.

## Common mix-ups

**Where is the tree UI?**  
Vision — not shipped.

**Is this the same as aptitudes?**  
No. Aptitudes are the twelve free-build stats live now.

**Do I need a wiki?**  
Design says catalogs are learnable and shared — no secret wiki lock.

**Souls only for trees?**  
Trees would spend souls; souls already have altar and tribute sinks.
