# Tribute on pacts

**Status:** Shipped  
**Loop:** Summon and fusion — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Creatures](../creatures.md)  
**HTML guide:** [site/mechanisms/tribute.html](../site/mechanisms/tribute.html)

---

## In one sentence

**Tribute** is the payment side of a pact — keep contracts paid. Overdue tribute is leverage against you.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Tribute** | What the pact asks you to pay to keep the books clear. Usually paid in souls — read the pact UI for the ask. |
| **Overdue** | You have not paid what is due. That state is leverage — you do not renegotiate your way out while it is owed. |
| **Leverage** | The contract holds power over you until the books are clear — not a separate currency. |
| **Pact** | The bond slot. Tribute is one duty of that bond — see [pacts and loyalty](pacts-loyalty.md). |

**Also true:**

- Loyalty can still decay under upkeep even when you understand tribute — both matter.
- Altar pulls also spend souls; plan the bank so tribute and summons do not starve each other.

---

## What it is

A pact is not free forever. Tribute is the recurring cost of keeping that demon under contract.

When tribute is overdue, renegotiation waits until you pay what you owe.

---

## How you use it

1. Open Pacts and read each contract’s tribute ask.
2. Pay when due if you still need that demon in the field.
3. If you are overdue, clear the debt before you expect friendly renegotiation.

---

## What it is not

It is not lawn-game sun.

It is not essence — fusion materials are a different stock.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. With a demon on a pact, open Pacts (`P`).
2. Find the tribute line and the due state.
3. Pay once when asked and confirm the overdue warning clears.

> Stop when overdue = leverage is clear. Fuse next: [specimen fusion](specimen-fusion.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Can I ignore tribute if loyalty looks fine?**  
Do not. Overdue tribute is its own problem — leverage stays until paid.

**Does tribute spend essence?**  
No. Plan around souls (and whatever the pact UI shows).

**If I release the demon, do I still owe?**  
Leaving a contract is a pact decision — clear dues the UI still shows before you assume you are free.

**Is this a real-money shop?**  
No. Local game stocks only.
