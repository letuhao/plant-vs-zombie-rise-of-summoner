## What it is (design)

You find domains out in the world (expedition discoveries are part of that story). From the Sanctum you pick a found domain and enter.

Inside: a room graph — fights, elites, caches, shrines, merchants, traps, wild talk. You manage a pack. You extract with a haul — or you wipe.

```text
  find domain  ->  Sanctum enter  ->  room graph
                                      |
                                      +->  extract with haul
                                      +->  wipe / fail
```

> Unlock (when it ships): first domain found on an expedition.

## Common mix-ups

**Can I enter a delve today?**  
No. Not finished / not playable yet.

**Is this just a long expedition?**  
No. Expeditions auto-resolve idle. Delves are a presence stage with rooms and extract.

**Where do I start one?**  
Design: find a domain, then enter from the Sanctum — when it ships.

**Do delves replace expeditions?**  
No. They are siblings.
