# Quest log

**Loop:** Quests and events — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)

## In one sentence

**Vision:** a layer you can open to track **quests** and **events** — a story clock, not only loot. Not finished / not playable yet.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Named so it cannot stay invisible — but you cannot open a finished quest log layer today. |
| **Quest log** | A player-facing layer for tracked quests and events — not the chronicle XP ledger. |
| **Story clock** | Narrative beats from expeditions, delves, and world events — opposite of a pure loot clock. |
| **Layer** | Opens over where you already are (like Almanac), not a travel stage like Delve. |

**Also true:**

- Expedition ticks that feed quests are Vision — see [expedition event ticks](expedition-event-ticks.md).
- Failure that opens a new questline hangs here and on farm / hunt / defend.

---

## Not finished / not playable yet

There is no finished player quest log layer in the current loop.

The catalog names it anyway so the story clock cannot be “forgotten” into loot-only design.

---

## What it is (design)

Track quests and events in one place you can open.

Feeds from delve quests, expedition ticks, world events, and turn reports as those systems ship.

```text
  expedition ticks / delve / world events
                    |
                    v
              quest log layer (Vision)
```

---

## What is not the quest log

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chronicle** | History and XP ledgers — ships as memory, not quest tracking. |
| **Almanac** | Dossiers and recipes from discovery — reference, not active quests. |

---

## What you do with this page

1. Play without waiting on a quest checklist UI — it is Vision.
2. Use Almanac and Chronicle for shipped memory surfaces.
3. When the log appears, treat it as a layer, not a new travel stage.

> Stop when quest log ≠ chronicle. Related: [world events](world-events.md), [failure branches](failure-branches.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Where is my quest log?**  
Vision — not finished / not playable yet.

**Is Chronicle the quest log?**  
No. Chronicle is history and progression ledgers.

**Do expeditions open quests today?**  
Full quest handoff from ticks is Vision.

**Is this a stage I travel to?**  
No. Design is a layer you open over where you are.
