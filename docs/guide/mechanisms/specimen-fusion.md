# Specimen fusion

**Status:** Shipped
**Loop:** Summon and fusion — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)
**Pillar:** [Creatures](../creatures.md)

## What it is

Merge specimens into stronger forms and discover recipes. The Fusion rail key (F) is this lab — not the host game's product name.

## How you use it

Open Fusion (F). Pick ingredients, spend essence when asked, learn recipes by trying merges.
