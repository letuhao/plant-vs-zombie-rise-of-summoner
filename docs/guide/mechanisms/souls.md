## In one sentence

**Souls** are the currency you earn by playing and spend to call new demons and keep them bound.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Soul** | A **currency number** in your save. Not a demon, not a life bar, not something you lose on death. |
| **Spend** | Souls leave your bank the moment you confirm at the [altar](summoning-altar.md). There is no refund. |
| **Bank** | One shared pool for the whole save. It is not per demon and not per world. |
| **Earn** | Play pays. Waiting does not — there is no idle soul trickle. |

**Also true:**

- Souls are one of three stocks. The others are [essence](essence.md) and [loam](loam.md), and they do not convert into each other.

---

## What it is

One bank, shown in the HUD wherever you are.

You watch it climb while you play and drop when you summon.

> Souls bank across worlds — see [New world](new-world-prestige.md).

---

## How it works

Three things touch the number:

```text
  play  ->  souls  ->  altar  ->  demons
                       +->  pacts
```

| Side | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Faucets** | Lawn matches, expedition returns, and map work pay souls. |
| **Sinks** | Altar pulls and pact upkeep spend them. |

| Where | What you get |
|---|---|
| Lawn match | Souls on a win, scaled by what you fought |
| Expedition | Souls in the return report |

---

## Where you find it

### In the HUD

The stock strip is always on screen.

1. Look at the top strip.
2. Souls sit beside essence.

> It updates without a refresh.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Play a lawn match to the end.
2. Open the [altar](summoning-altar.md).
3. Spend once, and read what the pull cost.

> Stop here. Pacts come next.

---

## Common mix-ups

**Do I lose souls when a demon dies?**  
No. Souls are a bank, not a health pool.

**Can I buy souls?**  
No. There is no store — this build is local and has no payments.

**Do souls decay?**  
No. Only [loam](loam.md) fades if you neglect ground.

**Is this the same as essence?**  
No. See [essence](essence.md).
