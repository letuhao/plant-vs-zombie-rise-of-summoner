| **Counter-development** | If you lean fire, the war grows fire-hard; if you lean summons, anti-summon shows up. Rebuild follows. |
| **Matched levels** | The rejected idea that enemy power is only “Dave’s level ± N.” This Vision is about strategy pressure, not a level treadmill. |

## Not finished / not playable yet

Play Zomboss and the map without waiting for automatic hard-counters to your aptitudes.

This page is the intended pressure curve — raise demons → a strategy works → the world counters → rebuild.

## What it is (design)

Your successful leans become the world’s homework.

The answer is not inflating a level number — it is changing resists, composition, and threats so yesterday’s easy plan stops being free.

```text
  lean works  ->  world notices  ->  counters appear  ->  you rebuild
```

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Not this** | Enemy level = Dave’s level. |
| **This** | Strategy answers strategy — fire lean meets fire-hard; summon lean meets anti-summon. |

## Where it would show up

### Across the war

When it ships, expect pressure through map threats and events — not a single toggle.

1. Keep building and fielding as you do now.
2. When counters appear, read them as content — then adjust roster and plan.
3. Until then, treat missing hard-counters as Vision.

> Not in the current loop yet.

## What you do with this page

1. Fight Zomboss and hold ground with the systems that ship today.
2. Do not assume the AI is broken because it does not hard-counter your lean yet.
3. When world events and counters arrive, rebuild on purpose — that is the fantasy.

> Stop when counter ≠ level sync. Related: [Zomboss](zomboss-commander.md), [world events](world-events.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Why isn’t the enemy hard-countering my fire team?**  
Counter-development is Vision. Absence is expected.

**Is this just higher numbers?**  
No. Design rejects matched-level padding as the answer.

**Is Zomboss Vision too?**  
No. Zomboss as commander ships. This is the later ecology of counters.

**Does this wipe my roster?**  
No. It pressures strategy so you rebuild plans — not a prestige wipe of who you are.
