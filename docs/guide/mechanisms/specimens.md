**Status:** Shipped
**Loop:** Summon and fusion — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)

## What it is

Every demon is an individual — level, gear, history — not a stack of copies. Duplicates have a job in fusion.

## How you use it

Open Creatures. Inspect the roster. Deploy bound specimens; send spare ones on expeditions.
