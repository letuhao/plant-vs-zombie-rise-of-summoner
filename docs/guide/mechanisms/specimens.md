## In one sentence

Every demon is a **specimen** — its own level, gear, and history — not a stack of identical copies.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Specimen** | One individual demon in your roster. Two Peashooter-line demons are still two specimens. |
| **Creature / demon** | Player words for the same roster beings. You collect creatures; the ones you summon and bind are demons. |
| **Duplicate** | Another specimen of a species you already have — fuel for fusion, not trash to delete on sight. |
| **Persistent** | They stay in your save across sessions and bank across worlds with who you are. |

**Also true:**

- Open Creatures (`C`) from the [layer rail](layer-rail.md).
- The full multi-tab creature sheet is still WIP — the roster already shows level, gear, and history.

---

## What it is

Identity, level, gear, traits, element, rarity, variant, and history live on the specimen.

Duplicates are not dust — they feed star merges, recipes, and trait inheritance in fusion.

---

## How you use the roster

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Inspect** | Open Creatures and read who you have. |
| **Bind** | Put demons on pacts so they will deploy — see [pacts and loyalty](pacts-loyalty.md). |
| **Field** | Bound specimens deploy to lawn; spare ones can go on expeditions. |
| **Fuse** | Spend duplicates and [essence](essence.md) in the Fusion lab. |

---

## Where specimens come from

Altar pulls spend souls.

Wild joins and (later) capture are other intakes — gacha is one path, never the only one.

```text
  altar / wild join / capture  ->  specimen on roster
                                      |
                                      +->  bind (pacts)
                                      +->  fuse (duplicates)
```

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Press `C` to open Creatures.
2. Pick one specimen and notice it has its own level and identity.
3. If you own a duplicate, treat it as fusion fuel — do not assume it is worthless.

> Stop when “stack of copies” no longer fits. Next: [summoning altar](summoning-altar.md) or [pacts](pacts-loyalty.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Can I merge two identical names into one stack?**  
No. They stay individuals until you fuse them on purpose.

**Is a specimen the same as a species?**  
Species is the kind. Specimen is one living instance of that kind.

**Do specimens wipe on a new world?**  
No. Roster banks with who you are — see [New world](new-world-prestige.md).

**Where is the full sheet?**  
Roster is Shipped; the finished multi-tab sheet is WIP.
