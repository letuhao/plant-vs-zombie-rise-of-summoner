# Expeditions

**Status:** Shipped
**Loop:** Idle expeditions — core forever — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)
**Pillar:** [Expeditions](../expeditions.md)

## What it is

Dispatch spare demons, wait (30 min to 20 h), collect. No stamina. Sibling of the Delve, not a throwaway.

## How you use it

Open Expeditions. Pick demons not on a live task. Dispatch a duration tier. Collect when ready.
