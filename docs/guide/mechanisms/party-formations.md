# Party formations and combo skills

**Status:** Vision  
**Loop:** Dungeon crawler — the Delve · Interactive battles — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)

## In one sentence

**Vision:** a second axis besides who is in the pack — **how they stand**, and a few **combination skills**. Not finished / not playable yet.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Not playable yet. Formations and combo skills are locked design for presence fights. |
| **Formation** | How the pack stands in a fight you play — not an expedition auto-resolve setting. |
| **Combo skill** | A combination skill that depends on who stands with whom — not a second element ring. |
| **Presence** | Matters in delves and interactive battles. Expeditions stay auto-resolve. |

**Also true:**

- Named combat reactions / combo recipes are a related Vision on the combat language — different catalog entry.
- Who is in the pack is still the first axis — formations do not replace roster choice.

---

## Not finished / not playable yet

You will not find a finished formation editor on the live delve or battle stage today — those stages are not in the loop yet.

Expeditions remain auto-resolve and do not need this axis.

---

## What it is (design)

Pick who goes. Then pick how they stand.

A short set of combination skills rewards intentional setups when you are playing the fight yourself.

```text
  who is in the pack  +  how they stand  +  combo skills
                         |
                         +->  delve / interactive battle (presence)
                         +->  expeditions stay auto-resolve
```

---

## Where it would matter

### Presence stages

Delves and interactive battles — not idle dispatch.

1. Provision a pack for a delve or battle.
2. Set formation when the UI exists.
3. Use combination skills when the fight opens those windows.

> Not in the current loop yet.

---

## What you do with this page

1. Keep expeditions auto-resolve — do not expect formation play there.
2. Treat missing formation UI as Vision, not a bug.
3. When presence fights ship, learn who + stance + combo as three readable choices.

> Stop when formation ≠ aptitude tree. Parent: [the Delve](delve.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Do expeditions use formations?**  
No. Expeditions stay auto-resolve.

**Is this the same as named reactions?**  
Related Vision on combat depth — formations are party stance; named reactions are learnable fight recipes.

**Can I set formations today?**  
No. Not finished / not playable yet.

**Does formation replace who I bring?**  
No. It is a second axis beside roster choice.
