## In one sentence

**Twelve aptitudes** — you have **no class**. Points go where you want, at one price.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Aptitude** | One of twelve free-build stats (Might, Fortitude, Vigor, …). Primary dials of a build. |
| **No class** | You do not pick Warrior/Mage. Classes exist as Zomboss AI patterns — enemy legibility, not your container. |
| **Posture** | Force, Finesse, Bastion — regions of build space, not locks that surcharge dabbling. |
| **Respec** | Changing your mind is allowed and priced when it applies — friction, not a forbidden door. |

**Also true:**

- In this build, commander-scope spend is live and thin; other scopes and a polished sheet are still ahead.
- Allocation can stack across commander, species, variant, and the unique demon — who you are is the sum.

## The twelve (feel)

| Aptitude | Feel |
|---|---|
| Might | Hit harder |
| Fortitude | Take less of everything |
| Vigor | More to lose before you lose |
| Onslaught | Their guard stops mattering |
| Agility | Be somewhere else |
| Composure | Nothing lands clean on you |
| Pierce | Armour stops mattering |
| Focus | Do it again, sooner, cheaper |
| Bulwark | Stop it outright, sometimes |
| Retribution | Hitting you costs them |
| Precision | They cannot dodge |
| Ferocity | Sometimes it is much worse |

## How you spend

1. Earn aptitude points as Dave progresses.
2. Open Builds / progression.
3. Spend freely across the twelve — no posture gate tax.

> Exact point costs are on screen / in balance data.

---

## What reaches the field

What you allocate at commander scope reaches every demon you field.

Species and specimen layers can add more lean later (species builds still WIP).

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Confirm you have aptitude points to spend.
2. Open builds and put points into two aptitudes that match how you want to play.
3. Field a demon and treat the change as side-wide commander lean.

> Stop when “no class” sticks. WIP lean: [species builds](species-builds.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Which class should I pick?**  
None. Free-build only. Enemy “classes” are Zomboss patterns.

**Do postures lock me out?**  
No. They describe space; they do not surcharge dabbling.

**Why is the sheet thin?**  
Commander spend is Shipped thin — fuller scopes still ahead.

**Is this the same as relics?**  
No. Aptitudes are free-build stats; relics are gear.
