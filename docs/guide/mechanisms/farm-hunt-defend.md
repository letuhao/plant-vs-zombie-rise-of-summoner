## In one sentence

**Farm / hunt / defend** names the territorial loop — yield and materials, prey and capture, Seat and homeworld — so it cannot hide under “the rift.”

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Farm** | Hold ground, take sector yield, expedition mats, later buildings. [Loam](loam.md) is position, not a wallet. |
| **Hunt** | Prey on the lawn, wild joins, roaming, delves, map fights — pressure that grows the roster and clears threats. |
| **Defend** | Fracture and neglect, Seat siege, homeworld as the spine you must not lose. |
| **Thin** | The verbs are real in this build (loam pressure, fog, claim), but full ecology, buildings, and sieges deepen later. |

**Also true:**

- Sieges and richer ecology are Vision. Sector buildings and wardens are WIP.
- Failure branches and enemy counter-development hang on this loop as Vision — consequence and pushback, not only a wipe.

## What it is

Three verbs for empire play on and around the rift.

They share stocks and stages: farm leans loam and holdings; hunt leans lawn, capture, delves; defend leans Fracture, Seat, and homeworld.

| Verb | Means |
|---|---|
| Farm | Hold ground, sector yield, expedition mats, later buildings |
| Hunt | Lawn prey, capture, wild joins, roaming, delves, map fights |
| Defend | Fracture and neglect, Seat siege, homeworld spine |

---

## How it works today

Shipped thin — real pressure, unfinished depth:

```text
  farm  ->  hold / yield / loam
  hunt  ->  lawn / capture / delves / map prey
  defend ->  Fracture / Seat / homeworld
```

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shipped** | Loam pressure, fog, claim, and map adventure already make holding ground matter. |
| **WIP** | Buildings and wardens harden the farm beat — see [sector buildings](sector-buildings.md) and [map orders](map-orders.md). |
| **Vision** | Sieges, richer ecology, counter-development, and failure branches. |

## Where you find it

### Across places

There is no single “farm mode” button — the verbs describe how you play.

1. On the world map: claim and hold for farm; watch loam fade for defend.
2. On the lawn and expeditions: hunt for prey, materials, and joins.
3. Treat homeworld safety as defend even while you expand.

> Status is Shipped (thin) — expect the shape, not the finished ecology.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Claim or hold a sector and notice loam pressure (farm / defend).
2. Play a lawn match or collect an expedition for hunt materials or joins.
3. Say out loud which verb you are doing — if you cannot, you are probably only marching.

> Stop when “the rift” unpacks into three verbs. Related: [loam](loam.md), [siege](siege.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Is farm a fourth currency?**  
No. Farm uses loam and materials — never a new wallet.

**Is hunt only delves?**  
No. Lawn, capture, wild joins, and map prey count. Delves are Vision depth.

**Is defend only combat?**  
No. Neglect and the Fracture are defend — ground can fail without a fight.

**Why does this feel unfinished?**  
It is Shipped thin. Buildings, sieges, and richer ecology are still ahead.
