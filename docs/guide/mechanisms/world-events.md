Blood moons, roaming bosses, and related ecology beats.

```text

```

## Common mix-ups

**Where are blood moons?**  
Vision — not finished / not playable yet.

**Is End Turn a world event?**  
No. End Turn is the virtual-turn clock. Events are story/ecology pushes.

**Are expedition injuries world events?**  
No. Those are expedition ticks. World events are the wider ecology clock.

**Is this the same as counter-development?**  
Sibling Vision. Events are scheduled/triggered pushes; counters are strategy answers.
