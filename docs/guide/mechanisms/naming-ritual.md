# First-run naming ritual

**Status:** WIP

## In one sentence

**WIP:** on a brand-new save, you will bind and name your first creature **in the Sanctum** so the roster starts as a story — not a spreadsheet dump.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **WIP** | Designed and partly forged — not finished. You will not get the full ritual yet. |
| **Ritual** | A short first-session beat in the hall, not a combat ability and not a paid ceremony. |
| **In place** | Naming happens in the Sanctum itself — you do not leave for a separate “create character” website. |
| **Roster** | Your specimens. The ritual is how the first one becomes yours with a name. |

**Also true:**

- Until it ships, you still land in the [Sanctum](sanctum.md) after [save select](save-select.md) and use the rail yourself.
- This is not the Dave-level chapter drip — see [unlock chapters](unlock-chapters.md).

---

## What it will feel like

A nearly empty hall on purpose.

You bind a first creature, give it a name, and get one clear next step — not a tour of every menu.

---

## What you do today

Create a summoner at the door.

Enter the Sanctum and open Creatures when you are ready.

Treat any early naming or binding UI as temporary until this ritual owns the first session.

> Do not wait for the ritual to play — the hall and rail are already open.

---

## Why it matters

Without a naming beat, the first screen becomes a list of systems.

With it, the war starts as “this demon is yours,” then the rail opens around that story.

---

## What you do while it is WIP

1. Finish save select and enter the Sanctum.
2. Open Creatures and meet whatever roster the build gives you.
3. Ignore missing “guided empty hall” moments — they are this page’s unfinished work.

> Stop when you know the ritual is coming, not broken. Home: [Sanctum](sanctum.md). Specimens: [persistent specimens](specimens.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Is my game broken because nothing forced me to name a demon?**  
No. The guided ritual is WIP. You can still play.

**Is this the same as save select?**  
No. Save select names the summoner. This ritual names the first creature.

**Will it lock the rail forever?**  
No. The design opens one clear next step, then the rest of the hall.

**Does Vision Dave 1 include this?**  
Dave 1 is lawn and Sanctum basics. The naming ritual is its own first-session WIP beat.
