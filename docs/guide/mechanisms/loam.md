# Loam and the Fracture

**Status:** Shipped
**Loop:** Farm / hunt / defend · World map — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)
**Pillar:** [The rift](../the-rift.md)

## What it is

Ground pressure on sectors you hold. Neglect fades holdings. Loam never banks across worlds — it is position, not a wallet.

## How you use it

Claim and hold sectors. Watch loam on the map. If you neglect ground, the Fracture takes it back.
