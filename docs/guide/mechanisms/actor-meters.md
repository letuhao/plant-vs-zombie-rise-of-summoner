## In one sentence

**WIP:** actors share six pools — HP, stamina, sun/hunger, spirit, yang/yin, poise — the fuels skills and stances will spend. Not finished yet.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **WIP** | Designed and partly built. Poise and the full skill-cost loop are not finished — do not expect every pool as a live spend UI yet. |
| **Meter** | A shared actor pool. Faction difference is a **label** (plant vs zombie wording), not a different system. |
| **Sun (two meanings)** | The **lawn sun bank** in a match, and the plant **label** for the hunger meter. Different scopes — do not mix them. |
| **Yang / Yin** | Plant / zombie labels for stance and buff fuel — not a morality meter. |
| **Poise** | The guard pool. When it lands, guard is a real stance — not a free shrug. |

**Also true:**

- Health already matters everywhere; the unfinished part is spending the other pools on skills.
- Skills that cost these meters are [skills and loadouts](skills-loadouts.md) — also WIP.

## Not finished yet

When meters land, skills cost something you can see, and guard is a real stance.

Until then, treat missing spend UI as unfinished work — not a broken save.

## The six pools

Same system; plant and zombie only rename some rows:

| Pool | Plant label | Zombie label |
|---|---|---|
| Health | HP | HP |
| Body fuel | Stamina | Stamina |
| Hunger | Sun | Hunger |
| Nerve | Spirit | Spirit |
| Stance / buff fuel | Yang | Yin |
| Guard | Poise | Poise |

> Do not confuse plant “Sun” on this table with the match’s lawn sun bank.

---

## What you do today

Play with HP and the combat language that already ships — elements, shields, statuses.

Read this page as the map of fuels coming with skills and interactive battles.

## What you do while it is WIP

1. Keep playing lawn and roster loops — meters are not a gate to start.
2. When meter UI appears, learn the six names and which label your side uses.
3. Separate lawn sun (match bank) from the plant hunger label named Sun.

> Paired WIP: [skills and loadouts](skills-loadouts.md) · [interactive battles](interactive-battles.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Where do I spend yang or poise?**  
Not finished yet. Poise and the skill-cost loop are WIP.

**Is plant “Sun” the same as match sun?**  
No. Match sun is the lawn bank. Sun on the meter table is the plant hunger label.

**Do plants and zombies use different meter systems?**  
No. Same six pools — labels differ.

**Is this a stamina gate on the whole game?**  
No. Design forbids a player stamina gate. These are actor combat pools.

**Do I need meters to finish a lawn match?**  
No. Lawn play and shipped combat language already work.
