## Common mix-ups

**Is this my class?**  
No. You still have no class. This is species lean.

**Does every specimen share one lean?**  
Design is per species line you field, not one global class.

**Can Zomboss use this against me?**  
Adaptive enemy patterns are content — yes, as opposing builds.
