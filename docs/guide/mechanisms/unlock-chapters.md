## In one sentence

**Vision:** complexity will drip by **Dave’s level** so day one is not a spreadsheet — this is not what gates the live rail today.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Locked design, not playable as a gate yet. You cannot turn this on in the current build. |
| **Dave’s level** | Your summoner’s endless level — see [Dave’s level](dave-level.md). Chapters would open features as that number rises. |
| **Chapter** | A drip of systems (items, pacts, map…) timed to a Dave threshold — not a story cutscene book. |
| **Live rail** | What you have now unlocks on [play beats](unlock-beats.md), not on this table. |

**Also true:**

- Thresholds will live in balance data later. Exact numbers below are the product map, not a promise that the live game already enforces them.
- Dave’s level has no cap. New worlds raise prestige; they do not wipe who you are.

## Not in the current loop

Open the rail today and you will see beat unlocks, not Dave-gated chapters.

## Why chapters exist (as design)

A full rail on minute one turns the hall into a spreadsheet.

Chapters keep lawn-first onboarding while still promising the whole war over time.

---

## What you do with this page

1. Treat the table as future design, not a checklist for today’s build.
2. Use [unlock beats](unlock-beats.md) for what the rail actually does now.
3. When you wonder “why isn’t X open at Dave 10?”, remember chapters are Vision.

> Stop when you can separate Vision chapters from live beats. Related power: [Dave’s level](dave-level.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**I reached Dave 20 — where are Expeditions?**  
Expeditions unlock on the live beat (bound demon), not on this Vision table yet.

**Are the Dave numbers final?**  
They are the product map. Balance data will own the live thresholds later.

**Does a new world reset Dave?**  
New worlds are prestige of place — you keep who you are. See [New world](new-world-prestige.md).

**Is this the same as unlock beats?**  
No. Beats are Shipped. Chapters are Vision.
