# Wild joins

**Status:** Shipped  
**Loop:** Summon and fusion · Idle expeditions — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Expeditions](../expeditions.md)  
**HTML guide:** [site/mechanisms/wild-joins.html](../site/mechanisms/wild-joins.html)

---

## In one sentence

**Wild joins** are demons that enter the roster from expedition outcomes — intake that is not the altar.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Wild join** | A specimen that arrives from an expedition result, not from a soul pull. |
| **Intake** | Any path that adds a demon. Altar, wild join, and (later) capture are different intakes. |
| **Expedition** | Idle squad mission — dispatch, wait, collect. Detail: [expeditions](expeditions.md). |
| **Altar** | Soul gacha pulls. Wild joins prove gacha is not the only path. |

**Also true:**

- After a wild join lands, treat it like any [specimen](specimens.md) — bind, fuse, or field as usual.
- Capture on the lawn/delve is a different WIP intake — see [capture](capture.md).

---

## What it is

Expeditions can return more than materials and souls.

A wild join is a full specimen appearing in Creatures when the outcome says so.

---

## How you use it

```text
  dispatch expedition  ->  wait  ->  collect  ->  wild join on roster
```

1. Dispatch an expedition with a squad.
2. Collect when it returns.
3. If a wild join is in the report, open Creatures and meet the new specimen.

---

## Why it exists

If only the altar grew the roster, idle play would never surprise you with a demon.

Wild joins keep expeditions on the summon-and-fusion spine.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Unlock Expeditions (bound demon available).
2. Send a squad and collect a return.
3. If no wild join this time, still read the report — joins are an outcome, not a guarantee every run.

> Stop when altar ≠ only intake. WIP hunt path: [capture](capture.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Did I get charged souls for a wild join?**  
No. That is the point of a non-altar intake.

**Is every expedition return a new demon?**  
No. Materials and souls are common; wild joins are one possible outcome.

**Is this capture?**  
No. Capture is WIP pressure-and-catch in a run.

**Can a wild join fuse later?**  
Yes. It is a normal specimen once it lands.
