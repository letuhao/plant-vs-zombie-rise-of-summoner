## In one sentence

**WIP:** weaken a wild demon in a run and attempt to **catch** it — a hunt intake beside the altar and wild joins.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **WIP** | Not finished. You should not expect a reliable capture button on every lawn yet. |
| **Capture** | Pressure a target in play, then attempt to add it as a specimen — not an altar pull. |
| **Wild** | A demon you meet in a run that is not already on your roster. |
| **Lawn-only finds** | Design allows a small slice of species as lawn finds (never legendaries forever gated that way), with web paths so web-only play can still finish milestones. |

**Also true:**

- Altar and [wild joins](wild-joins.md) already grow the roster without capture.
- Vision ecology (raids, blood moons) is separate — see the creatures pillar.

---

## What it will feel like

Fight or pressure a target on the lawn or in a delve.

Attempt capture when the window opens — success adds a specimen.

```text
  meet wild target  ->  weaken / pressure  ->  capture attempt  ->  specimen
```

---

## What you do today

Play lawn and expeditions with the intakes that already ship.

Treat missing capture prompts as unfinished work, not a broken save.

---

## Design guardrails

No legendary forever locked behind capture-only.

Web-only players still need a path to finish milestones.

---

## What you do while it is WIP

1. Use the altar and wild joins to grow the roster now.
2. When capture UI appears in a build, follow on-screen pressure and attempt rules.
3. Do not plan a legendary-only-from-capture collection route.

> Roster category intakes end here for now. Power next: [Dave’s level](dave-level.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Why can’t I capture on this lawn?**  
Capture is WIP — absence is expected.

**Is capture the same as wild join?**  
No. Wild joins come from expedition reports; capture is in-run pressure.

**Will legendaries be capture-only?**  
No. Design forbids gating legendaries forever this way.

**Does capture spend souls like the altar?**  
Do not assume altar pricing. When it ships, trust the attempt UI.
