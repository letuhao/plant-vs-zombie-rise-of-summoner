# Pacts and loyalty

**Status:** Shipped  
**Loop:** Summon and fusion — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Creatures](../creatures.md)  
**HTML guide:** [site/mechanisms/pacts-loyalty.html](../site/mechanisms/pacts-loyalty.html)

---

## In one sentence

**Pacts** bind demons to contract slots. Keep **loyalty** up — neglect a bound demon long enough and it **refuses to deploy**.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Pact** | A contract slot that binds a specimen for field work. Opened with `P` on the rail. |
| **Loyalty** | How willing that bound demon is to keep working for you. It decays under daily upkeep if you ignore the pact. |
| **Refuse to deploy** | The demon stays in the roster but will not go to the lawn (or other field work) until loyalty is healthy again. |
| **Bound** | Assigned to a pact slot. Bound demons are the ones you field; spare unbound ones can still sit on expeditions. |

**Also true:**

- Tribute is part of keeping a pact paid — see [tribute](tribute.md).
- Deploy detail for the lawn: [bound demon deploy](lawn-deploy.md).

---

## What it is

Binding is how a specimen becomes someone you can count on in a fight.

Loyalty is the trust meter on that bond — not a second health bar.

---

## How you use it

```text
  specimen  ->  pact slot  ->  loyalty healthy  ->  can deploy
                     |
                     +->  neglect  ->  refuse to deploy
```

1. Open Pacts (`P`).
2. Assign a demon to a contract slot.
3. Watch loyalty and pay what the pact asks (including tribute).
4. Deploy bound demons when the lawn or other field work needs them.

---

## Why it exists

Without loyalty, the roster is a free storage chest.

With it, binding is a relationship you maintain — not a one-click permanent buff.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Open Pacts when the layer is unlocked (a contract has been offered).
2. Bind one demon you actually want on the lawn.
3. Note loyalty and any tribute ask before you walk away for a long time.

> Stop when “refuse to deploy” is clear. Pay next: [tribute](tribute.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Did my demon get deleted when loyalty dropped?**  
No. It refuses to deploy. Fix the pact; the specimen is still yours.

**Can unbound demons never fight?**  
Spare demons can still go on expeditions. Lawn deploy wants a healthy bound pact.

**Is loyalty the same as tribute?**  
No. Loyalty is willingness. Tribute is the payment side of the contract — see [tribute](tribute.md).

**Do I bind every demon?**  
Only as many as you need in the field. Duplicates and spares have other jobs (fusion, expeditions).
