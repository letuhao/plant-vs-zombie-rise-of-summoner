# New world — keep who you are

**Status:** Shipped (fiction)
**Loop:** Level up and power · Farm / hunt / defend — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)
**Pillar:** [The game](../the-game.md)

## What it is

You keep who you are; you lose where you were. Roster, souls, and essence bank; loam and failed-map holdings do not. Ending a world is progression, not a prestige wipe of the summoner.

## How you use it

Win or lose a world per The game. Start another with your roster and stocks that bank. Loam resets with the map.
