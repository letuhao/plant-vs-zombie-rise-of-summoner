## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **WIP** | Deep craft is not a finished player loop. |
| **Affix** | Rolled properties frozen at roll — gear identity, not a random re-roll every glance. |
| **Enhance / reroll** | Design costs something real instead of pure luck spam. |
| **Named unique** | A relic that breaks the generator’s usual rules on purpose. |

- Lives inside [relics armoury](relics-armoury.md).

**Is crafting free rerolls?**

**Same as fusion?**  
No. Fusion merges specimens; crafting is gear.

Some sinks may ask stocks later — trust UI when it ships; do not invent a recipe here.
