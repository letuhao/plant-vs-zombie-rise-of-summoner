## In one sentence

**Vision:** the war presented in three layers — rift graph, sector board, lane board. Not finished / not playable yet as that full stack.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Vision** | Locked design. You play the graph today — not the finished three-layer presentation. |
| **Rift graph** | Sectors and lanes — adventure. What you play today. |
| **Sector board** | Inside one sector: slots, construction, your base — empire stage depth. |
| **Lane board** | Five-lane combat when a fight is tested — the battle presentation layer. |

**Also true:**

- Map→battle handoff is the WIP bridge into fights; lane board is the Vision presentation of that fight.
- Do not invent a fourth layer or a diary clock to glue them.

## Not finished / not playable yet

The graph is live. Sector board and lane board as a finished three-layer fantasy are not.

Play [world map](world-map.md) now; treat missing boards as Vision.

## What it is (design)

Three layers of the war:

```text
  [ rift graph ]  ->  [ sector board ]  ->  [ lane board ]
   adventure           empire stage           fight test
```

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| Rift graph | Sectors and lanes — where you go (adventure) |
| Sector board | Inside one sector — slots, construction, base (empire) |
| Lane board | Five-lane combat when a fight is tested |

---

## How it hangs on live work

Graph + End Turn + fog ship today.

Buildings and map→battle handoff harden the middle and the fight bridge as WIP.

---

## What you do with this page

1. Learn the three names so later UI does not feel like three different games.
2. Play the shipped graph without waiting on sector/lane boards.
3. When fights you play appear, expect them to feed back to the graph — see [map battle handoff](map-battle-handoff.md).

> Stop when three layers ≠ three unrelated modes. Related: [world stage](world-stage.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Can I open the sector board today?**  
Not as the finished Vision board. Thin graph play is what you have.

**Is lane board the lawn?**  
No. Lawn is its own first core loop. Lane board is map/battle presentation.

**Do I need all three to win?**  
Win/lose hang on fortress and homeworld. Layers are how the war is shown, not three win conditions.

**Is the generator required for three layers?**  
Related Vision work — see [world generator](world-generator.md) — but layers are a presentation idea of their own.
