# Title / save select

**Status:** Shipped

## In one sentence

At the **door** you meet the title screen, then **choose or create a summoner save** — that save is the world you keep.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The first control-room screen after the launcher opens the browser — Rise of Summoner’s front door, not the lawn game’s main menu. |
| **Save / summoner** | One local profile for your whole campaign: roster, stocks, map progress. Picked once at the door, not from a dropdown on every screen. |
| **Continue** | Resume with the summoner the control room already has selected. Disabled until you have created at least one save. |
| **Lawn-game save** | The Plants vs. Zombies pack’s own save files. Rise of Summoner does not rewrite them. This page is only about RPG saves. |

**Also true:**

- Saves live on your machine beside the local helper — no account, no cloud. See [local control room](local-control-room.md).
- Updating Rise of Summoner is meant to keep this data.

## What you see — title

Centered title: Rise of Summoner, with a short Plants vs. Zombies line underneath.

Buttons: Continue, New summoner, Saves, Settings, and Quit (Quit may be unavailable in the browser-only path).

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Continue** | Goes straight to the Sanctum with the current summoner. Greyed out if you have never created one. |
| **New summoner** | Opens save select already ready to name a new profile. |
| **Saves** | Opens the full choose-a-summoner list. |
| **Settings** | Opens System over Sanctum — same settings surface you reach with Esc later. |

---

## What you see — choose a summoner

Heading: Choose a summoner.

Each slot shows the name, when it was created, and a world seed for that save.

Continue on a slot selects it and lands you in the Sanctum. + New summoner asks for a name, creates the save, then continues.

> Back to title returns to the door without changing who is selected.

---

## Why the door exists

A save is chosen once, at the door — not from a picker glued to the top of every layer.

That keeps Sanctum feeling like a place you are in, not a settings page that happens to have a game under it.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. From the title, pick New summoner (or Saves if you already have one).
2. Type a summoner name and Create — or Continue on an existing slot.
3. Land in the Sanctum. You are past the door.

> Stop here when you know which summoner you are playing. Next: [Sanctum](sanctum.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Is this my Plants vs. Zombies save?**  
No. This is the Rise of Summoner summoner profile. The lawn pack keeps its own saves.

**Do I need an online account?**  
No. Local only — see [local control room](local-control-room.md).

**Where did the player picker on every screen go?**  
On purpose. You pick at the door; you do not re-pick from a HUD dropdown all session.

**What is the seed under my name?**  
A label for this save’s world root. You can see it; you do not need to type it to play.

**Can I have more than one summoner?**  
Yes. Create another slot from Saves or New summoner, then Continue on the one you want.
