# Type and specimen XP

**Status:** Shipped  
**Loop:** Level up and power — see [The loops](../the-loops.md)  
**Pillar:** [Almanac](../almanac.md)

---

## In one sentence

Plants, zombies, and demons grow from **work they actually did** — type lines and individual specimens, not only Dave’s level.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Specimen XP** | Progress on one demon instance — that Peashooter-line specimen you fielded. |
| **Type XP** | Progress on a species / type line you have used and met — feeds how deep that line feels over time. |
| **Almanac** | Dossiers for what you have met. Meeting types deepens reference — see [almanac](almanac.md). |
| **Chronicle** | Your run and progression history — a place XP and levels show up. |

**Also true:**

- Dave’s level is a separate summoner line — see [Dave’s level](dave-level.md).
- Field units and specimens; progress shows on Chronicle / roster sheets.

---

## What it is

Power is not only “Dave went up.”

Who you fielded and what you met also grow — individuals and type memory.

---

## How you use it

1. Deploy and play with real specimens.
2. Check roster sheets and Chronicle for their progress.
3. Meet new types in play to deepen almanac dossiers.

---

## Why both tracks

Specimen XP rewards the individuals you raise.

Type XP rewards learning and fielding a line — almanac and builds both care.

---

## What you do (first time)

1. Field a bound demon on a lawn or expedition.
2. Afterward, open Creatures and notice that specimen’s progress.
3. Open Almanac or Chronicle and see type / history catch up.

> Stop when Dave ≠ specimen XP is clear. Spend power: [aptitudes](aptitudes.md).

---

## Common mix-ups

**Does only Dave’s level matter?**  
No. Specimens and types grow from work they did.

**If I never field a demon, does it level?**  
Progress comes from work done — idle shelf pets stay behind.

**Is type XP the same as fusion rarity?**  
No. Rarity and fusion are other systems; this is growth from play.

**Where do I read it?**  
Roster sheets, Chronicle, and Almanac dossiers.
