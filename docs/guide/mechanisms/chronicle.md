## In one sentence

**Chronicle** is your history — run results, progression, and the lasting sheet of what you earned and spent.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **Chronicle** | The history layer (`H`) — ledgers and run memory. Not the Almanac dossier browser. |
| **Progress survives** | Nothing you earn disappears with the level. There is no hard ceiling that says the grind is over. |
| **XP ledger** | Tracks summoner, plant types, zombie types, and specimens — separate from the souls bank. |
| **Run result** | A recorded outcome of play — part of history, not a quest checklist. |

**Also true:**

- Souls buy summons and rituals; XP grows power curves and unlocks — different stocks of progress.
- New worlds keep who you are — chronicle memory hangs with that prestige of place.

## What it is

Your history of the war: what you ran, what you earned, what leveled.

It is the ledger of your summoner story — not a wiki of species art.

---

## How progression lands

```text
  play  ->  XP / run results  ->  Chronicle (H)
  play  ->  souls (separate bank)
```

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Run results** | History of what you finished. |
| **Progression dossier** | The lasting sheet of earned and spent power. |

| Track | What grows |
|---|---|
| Summoner | Your account level and XP |
| Plant / zombie types | Per-type levels from play |
| Specimens | Individual demons from work they did — lawn, expedition, battle |

## What you do (first time)

1. Finish a lawn match or expedition that can grant XP.
2. Open Chronicle (`H`) and find a ledger that moved.
3. Note that souls did not replace XP — both can rise for different jobs.

> Stop when chronicle ≠ almanac. Prestige of place: [new world](new-world-prestige.md).

## Common mix-ups

**Is Chronicle the quest log?**  
No. Quest log is Vision. Chronicle is history and XP.

**Do I lose XP when a world ends?**  
You keep who you are — progression banks with the summoner. Loam and map holdings do not.

**Is there a level cap?**  
No hard ceiling — magnitudes keep meaning as you climb.

**Are souls shown as XP?**  
No. Souls are a separate stock from XP ledgers.
