## In one sentence

**WIP:** turn-based fights on a **battle stage** you play yourself — initiative, action bar, targets, wind-up and interrupt feel. Not finished yet; many web fights still auto-resolve.

---

## Blind spots (if you are new)

These words get guessed wrong. Read them once before the rest of the page.

| Word | What it actually means here |
|---|---|
| **WIP** | Not finished. You do not yet pilot every turn on a shipped battle stage. |
| **Interactive** | You pick targets and spend actions. Different from watching an auto-resolve report. |
| **Auto-resolve** | Expeditions and many web fights still resolve with the same combat math without you clicking each turn. |
| **Battle stage** | The place you fight hands-on — not the lawn board and not the rift map itself. |
| **Same math** | Auto-resolve and interactive use the same fight language. You feel the roster either way; only the pilot changes. |

**Also true:**

- Skills and meters that feed the action bar are also WIP — see [skills and loadouts](skills-loadouts.md) and [actor meters](actor-meters.md).
- Named recipe catalog stays Vision — [named reactions](named-reactions.md).

## Not finished yet

Today you feel the roster through auto-resolve more often than through a turn bar you own.

When the battle stage ships, you pilot turns: initiative track, action bar, targets, reactions and counters.

---

## What it will feel like

Design shape from the combat pillar:

```text
  roster (same)  ->  auto-resolve (today, many fights)
                 ->  battle stage (WIP)  ->  you pilot turns
```

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Initiative** | Who acts when on the track. |
| **Action bar** | What you can spend this turn. |
| **Targets** | You pick who the action hits. |
| **Reactions** | Windows to interrupt, counter, or answer — feel first; named catalog later. |

---

## Where it hangs

Delves and map fights want this stage. Expeditions can stay auto-resolve on purpose.

Map handoff into battle is its own WIP — [map → battle handoff](map-battle-handoff.md).

---

## What you do while it is WIP

1. Keep sending expeditions and reading returns — auto-resolve is intentional for idle.
2. Build roster power; the same math will matter when you pilot.
3. When a battle stage appears in a build, follow on-screen initiative and targeting — do not expect the lawn controls.

> Lawn remains the first core board match — [lawn match](lawn-match.md). This page is the hands-on stage elsewhere.

---

## Common mix-ups

**Why don’t I get turns on expeditions?**  
Expeditions can stay auto-resolve. Interactive battles are a different place.

**Is the lawn becoming turn-based?**  
No. The lawn stays a live board match. This is a separate battle stage.

**Does auto-resolve use weaker math?**  
No. Same combat language — you just are not piloting each action.

**Where is the battle stage button?**  
WIP — not finished. Absence is expected.

**Are named combo recipes required first?**  
No. Reaction windows can ship with battles; the named catalog is Vision.
