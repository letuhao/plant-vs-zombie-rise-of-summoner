# PvZ canon boundary for onboarding

**Purpose:** give a future Rise of Summoner onboarding story a truthful PvZ base without laundering the project's Rift/Fracture/Void setting into franchise canon.

**Research date:** 2026-09-14  
**Scope:** original *Plants vs. Zombies* (2009) and the international *Plants vs. Zombies 2* continuity. This is a compact narrative reference, not a series-wide canon encyclopedia.

## The Gnomes: the real high-cosmos PvZ thread

The “dwarfs” in the request are almost certainly the **Gnomes** from *Plants vs. Zombies: Garden Warfare 2*—a separate shooter-continuity story thread, not the PvZ2 taco trip.

| Observed story element | What the evidence supports | What it does **not** support |
|---|---|---|
| **Infinity Time / Gnomiverse** | The shipped mode sends plants or zombies into Infinity to fight Gnomes and their leader, Gnomus. Its mode description calls it the land of Gnomes, “Neither here nor there, nay not anywhere.” | That every PvZ game shares a formally defined multiverse cosmology. |
| **Gnomus the Gnome King** | Gnomus commands the Gnomes against Infinite Robots and speaks as their king. The source also records game quotes about “the hands of time,” “the Gnomiverse,” and gnomish power over time and space. | A formal franchise-wide ranking that calls him the single strongest entity. |
| **Cosmic-scale language** | The recorded Gnomus dialogue says, among other hyperbolic claims, “We can put time and space in our pocket,” refers to a “3rd infinity,” calls out singularities, and says Gnome Floaties destabilize time. | A demonstrated feat of destroying, resetting, or wiping out a multiverse. The reviewed material contains no such claim. |
| **Threat model in the game** | Gnomes are hostile to both plants and zombies in Infinity Time, use time-related hazards, and Gnome Kings can drain the mode's Time Stability. | That they are the cause of all plant–zombie conflict, the Rift, the Void, or a world-ending event in this project. |

### How to use them accurately

They are a strong canonical precedent for **weird cosmic/time imagery** in the Garden Warfare branch of PvZ. A future Rise of Summoner story can credibly take inspiration from that strangeness, but it must label any multiverse-ending threat, hierarchy, or connection to the Rift/Void as new project fiction.

Do not call the Gnomes “dwarfs” in production copy unless a localization specifically requires it; the English game and community references name them **Gnomes**.

### Tone implication

The franchise premise can handle danger, but its foundation is deliberately comic: a lawn defense, an eccentric inventor, a taco motive, a talking time machine, and absurd zombies. A user’s first contact should preserve that readable warmth and visual wit before it asks them to process high-stakes original lore.

## What Rise of Summoner adds

The following terms are part of this repository's product vision, not verified Plants vs. Zombies canon. They are currently documented in [The game](../../guide/the-game.md), [The Rift](../../guide/the-rift.md), and [The loops](../../guide/the-loops.md).

| Project term or claim | Required framing |
|---|---|
| A **Rift** or **Fracture** connecting sectors/timelines | Rise of Summoner's alternate setting. Do not say this is what happened in PvZ2. |
| Zomboss's time machine shattering the multiverse or scattering shards through time and space | Rise of Summoner's original inciting incident. Unverified as PvZ canon. |
| Plant-and-zombie fusions becoming **demons** | Rise of Summoner's original creature premise. Not franchise canon established by the sources reviewed. |
| Dave having a singular **homeworld** that must be defended | Rise of Summoner's original campaign structure. |
| The **Void**, chaos/corruption as a cosmic enemy, or an existential multiverse war | Rise of Summoner's original tone and terminology. Do not attribute it to PopCap/EA or to the PvZ2 opening. |
| Penny moving legions, shards, or an empire between worlds | A project-system extrapolation, not a verified PvZ ability or plot point. |

## Explicitly rejected wording

Do **not** describe any of these as a fact of PvZ lore without new primary evidence:

- “Zomboss shattered the multiverse.”
- “Rift shards created plant/zombie demons.”
- “The Void corrupts the PvZ universe.”
- “Crazy Dave's homeworld is under threat.”
- “Penny carries armies between eras.”
- “The Gnomes can erase the PvZ multiverse.”

The project can still use these ideas. It just has to say what they are: a Rise of Summoner continuation, an alternate reality, or a new crisis growing out of the familiar lawn-and-time-travel setup.

## Constraints for a future teaser or first-user guide

1. **Lead with recognition.** A lawn, plants, zombies, Dave, and Penny need little exposition for a PvZ player.
2. **Keep Dave recognizably Dave.** His helpful competence and weird humor are more faithful than solemn speeches or lore dumps.
3. **Make the handoff visible.** One concise beat can signal: “this part is our new story.” Do not quietly rewrite franchise history.
4. **Show the unfamiliar conflict before naming it.** If the Rift/Fracture is used, show a wrong-looking tear or fused silhouette first; reserve terminology for an optional detail or later scene.
5. **Keep the player’s first action concrete.** The canonical model is immediate defense of a lawn, not a lecture about cosmology.

## Research gaps

- This pass did not capture original PvZ or PvZ2 dialogue directly from the shipped games. Any quote or scene-order claim used in a production script needs a primary capture first.
- Search-result snippets were used only to locate sources; they are not evidence in this document.
- The community wiki is useful but not official EA/PopCap material. It should not be presented as a primary source.
- Spin-offs, comics, Chinese releases, and later PvZ3 continuities are intentionally excluded here. They should be researched as separate continuities if they become story inputs.
