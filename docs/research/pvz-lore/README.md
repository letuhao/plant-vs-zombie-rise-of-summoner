# Plants vs. Zombies lore research

This folder is a source-labelled reference for writing **Rise of Summoner** material that touches Plants vs. Zombies. Its first job is negative: it prevents franchise canon, community summaries, and this project's original fiction from being presented as the same thing.

Start with [Canon boundary and onboarding](canon-boundary-and-onboarding.md). It records the small set of narrative anchors verified for this pass, the evidence standard used for each, and the claims a future onboarding story must label as Rise of Summoner fiction.

## Scope

This is not an exhaustive franchise timeline. Plants vs. Zombies has separate continuities, regional releases, comics, and spin-offs. The immediate product need is narrower: an accurate foundation for the original 2009 game and the international release of *Plants vs. Zombies 2*, because this project invokes Crazy Dave, Penny, lawns, plants, zombies, and historical eras.

## Reading the labels

| Label | Meaning | How it may be used |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier A — primary canon** | A shipped game, its dialogue, or official PopCap/EA material directly observed or captured. | May be called franchise canon for the identified game or continuity. |
| **Tier B — community reference** | A community-maintained page that summarizes official content. | Useful for orientation and URLs, but not a substitute for a primary capture. |
| **Tier C — project fiction** | Rise of Summoner's own setting and terminology. | Present it as an alternate continuation or overlay, never as a fact from PvZ. |
| **Tier D — unverified** | A claim with no suitable source in this pass. | Do not use it as lore. Research it before writing. |

## Maintenance rule

When adding a lore claim, add its source URL, retrieval date, source class, and the exact claim it supports. If a future story needs dialogue-level fidelity, capture the relevant shipped scene first; do not promote a search-result snippet, fan theory, or wiki inference to canon.
