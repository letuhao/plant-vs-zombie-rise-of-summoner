World & Commitments

The world map is a stage, not a panel — a place the player acts in over time. Selecting a sector opens an inspector, because inspecting is looking. Around it sit the two layers that spend time and creatures: Expeditions and Pacts.

A · The world stage

Reached by travel from the sanctum's map table, or M. Time advances on the player's commit, never on wall-clock — so the map is never a screen that punishes you for reading it.

Every colour here is a token. The current world feature hardcodes a framework palette — #34d399, #a78bfa, #0f172a — which is a second design system living inside the newest surface. Lanes, ownership and fog all resolve to the theme. GG-29
Fog is a state of a node, not a separate thing. An unscouted sector renders at reduced opacity with a dashed border and keeps its position, so the shape of the world is legible before its contents are.

The disabled button carries its reason inline. "Renegotiate" is off and the sentence beside it says why — one of the three declared forbidden combinations, not a mystery. An unexplained disabled control is indistinguishable from a broken one. GG-55 · IA §6
