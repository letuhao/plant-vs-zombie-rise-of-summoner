Chronicle & Almanac

The two layers read at leisure rather than glanced at under pressure — so by GG-60 this is where the fiction is allowed to win. The Almanac is a book. The Chronicle is a record. Neither is a dashboard, and both are the home of numbers that must be explainable, not merely shown.
