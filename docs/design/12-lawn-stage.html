J · What this plate settles

  • Two shared entity widgets: ActorCollection and ActorSheet. Lawn docks compose them.
  • Character sheet landing is paper-doll + six pools + named primaries + live combat + equipped orders. Tabs are depth.
  • General vs unique is one sheet with locks, not two sheets.
  • Commander never occupies a tile; orders are an adventure bar.
  • Match sun bank and actor Sun (hunger) are different clusters.
  • No typeId / ptr on player chrome.
  • No new top-level route. Canvas survives every panel.

Owner questions (spec §14): always-list on singleton cells; action-bar caster = commander only; wild Paths = read-only species tree.
