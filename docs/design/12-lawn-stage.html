J · What this plate settles

  • Two shared entity widgets: ActorCollection (new; consumers include Creatures) and ActorSheet (replaces actor-sheet-shell landing). Lawn docks compose them after an occupant adapter.
  • ActorSheet is a no-scroll floor with eight tabs. Complete catalogs: plate 13. AptitudeTile is not a number box. Shield is up to 3 layers. Status mastery ≠ live stacks.
  • General vs unique is one sheet with locks. Chips are Fielded / Wave. Wild is capture.
  • Commander never occupies a tile; orders are an off-board combat book. Intent enqueue, no FE range.
  • Match sun bank and actor Sun (hunger) are different clusters. Cost chips name the payer.
  • No typeId / ptr on player chrome. ?sel= is instanceId only. GG-41 may keep the dump.
  • Dock is a reserved left column; 12 lawn columns stay visible. Canvas survives every panel. Unity does not pause because a sheet is open.
