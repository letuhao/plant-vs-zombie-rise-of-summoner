preset-distribution-chart

Live recharts donut fiction (SVG stand-in) of twelve aptitudes as %. Segment paint from posture theme packs — not a peer pie/radar. Honest empty when no points. Spec: docs/architecture/aptitude-sheet/spec-aptitude-pieces.md.

Structure (live)

  • Force group
  • Finesse group
  • Bastion group
  • Leftover 10%

Optional small posture stacked bar companion — not a second pie.
