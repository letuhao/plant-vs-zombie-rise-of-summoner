Actor HUD — per-unit readout on the lawn

A reusable, extensible HUD on each unit — icon, level, color, shape, and gradient — so the player can read what a unit is and what is happening to it without opening a sheet or parsing a wall of text. Unity attaches the stack at center-bottom (UnitFrame Body + worldYOffset). This plate is the visual guide and user-facing legend; the architecture ideal lives in actor-hud-ideal.md.
