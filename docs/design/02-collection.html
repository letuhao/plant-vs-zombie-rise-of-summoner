The loss column is not optional. Fusion destroys levelled creatures; a screen that shows only the result is selling, not informing. Note also the honest projection — the result is weaker right now and stronger later, and saying so is the difference between a player who trusts the screen and one who does not.
Fusion is one of the three declared forbidden combinations: a creature that is deployed cannot be fused or released, because the specimen is live in a run and the act has no reversal. The card in that state shows the reason rather than a dead button. GG-55 · IA §6

E · What this plate settles

QuestionAnswer
How is a creature chosen?From cards with art, name, element and the two numbers that matter — never a typed id
Where does comparison live?Beside the candidate, always, whenever a choice is being made
What does a destructive action show?Both columns — what is lost and what is gained — before the button
What happens at 1000 items?Search-first; the grid never renders unbounded
Do filters persist?Yes, for the session, as part of the layer's state
