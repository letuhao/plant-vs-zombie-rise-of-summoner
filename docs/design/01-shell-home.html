Shell & Sanctum

The door and the home stage. Everything a player meets before they are playing, and the place every run returns to. This plate answers decision D1 — home is the Sanctum — and shows what that means at the two moments that matter most: a brand-new save, and a save with everything unlocked.

A · Title

Band −1. One of only two surfaces that replace a stage without being travel. It exists to say what this is, and to get out of the way.

Rise of SummonerPlants vs. Zombies
v1.0.0 · offline ready

1440 × 810 viewport. The overlay sizes itself to the game window, so the title composes from the centre outward and never assumes a width.

Continue is first and largest. The overwhelmingly common intent is "carry on". Everything else is a ghost button. Note what is absent: no server address, no connection state, no build diagnostics beyond a version string in the corner. GG-2

The focus card is the only element that changes what it is. It answers "what should I do now" from state — overdue tribute beats a returned expedition beats a fusable pair beats "start a run". When nothing is pending it becomes the run prompt, never an empty box.

D · First run — the same stage, authored

A brand-new save is not this screen with everything empty. It is a written beginning: one creature, one instruction, and everything else genuinely absent. GG-43 · GG-44

This one answered

A sunflower has bound itself to you. It will remember what it learns and it will come back after every night. Give it a name.

Six of the eight rail entries are locked, and visibly so. Not hidden — a player who sees the shape of the game wants to reach it — and not present-but-dead. Hovering a locked entry says what unlocks it. This is why GG-44 is an architectural rule: a rail whose entries are a constant list cannot draw this screen at all.

Fusion

Hold two creatures of one species to unlock the fusion altar.

The badge carries a count. A bare dot says "something happened" and forces the player to go and look — which is the friction that trains people to ignore badges.
